COMMERCIAL LEASE — EXCERPT (Articles 4, 7, 9)

4. RENT
4.1 Tenant shall pay Base Rent of $18,400 per month in advance on the first day
    of each calendar month.
4.2 Any installment not received by the fifth business day of the month shall
    bear a late charge of 1.5% of the overdue amount per month until paid.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIR
7.1 Tenant shall keep the Premises in good order and repair and shall cure any
    maintenance deficiency within thirty (30) days after written notice from
    Landlord. If Tenant fails to cure, Landlord may perform the work and Tenant
    shall reimburse Landlord's reasonable costs plus a ten percent (10%)
    administrative fee.
7.2 Tenant shall not make structural alterations without Landlord's prior
    written consent. The consequence of an unconsented alteration is not
    specified in this excerpt.

9. INSURANCE
9.1 Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of not less
    than $2,000,000 per occurrence throughout the Term.
9.2 Tenant shall deliver certificates of insurance before the Commencement Date
    and within ten (10) days after each renewal.
9.3 Failure to maintain the required insurance is an Event of Default under
    Article 15; the specific remedy is set out there and is not reproduced in
    this excerpt.
