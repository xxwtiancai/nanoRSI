# Source figures and research images / 原文图与研究图片

Each image in this folder is tied to one catalogue record through [`manifest.json`](manifest.json) and `catalog.json`. `paper-figure` files are standalone figures extracted from the cited paper HTML; `paper-figure-crop` files are cropped from a cited PDF page to retain only the figure; `official-report-figure`, `official-project-figure`, `official-report-image` and `official-report-screenshot-crop` files come from the cited first-party research page or report. The detail pages preserve the figure number/page locator and link back to the source.

本目录的每张图片都通过 [`manifest.json`](manifest.json) 和 `catalog.json` 对应到一条资料记录。`paper-figure` 是从所引论文 HTML 提取的独立原图；`paper-figure-crop` 是从所引 PDF 页面裁剪、只保留图本身；其他 `official-*` 类型来自所引第一方研究页或报告。详情页会保留图号/页码定位，并回链到来源。

The files are retained as research references and explanatory material. PDF page captures are cropped to remove surrounding title, author, body-text and page-number regions. They are not evidence of a nanoRSI reproduction. Some papers and reports have different reuse terms; check the source and license before redistributing an image or using it in commercial marketing. No unrelated concept illustrations are included.

这些图片用于研究参考和机制说明。PDF 页面截图已裁剪掉标题、作者、正文和页码区域，不代表 nanoRSI 已经复现。不同论文和报告的再利用条款可能不同，重新发布或用于商业宣传前请核对来源与许可。本目录不包含无关概念插画。
