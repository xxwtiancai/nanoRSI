**Evaluation standard (2026-09-21):** experiments intended as effect evidence run on tasks and metrics from established upstream suites or community-standard validation sets, scored on frozen held-out panels under matched budgets; self-authored micro-panels (four-case demos, custom splits, synthetic clusters) are platform-validation records and are not presented as effect claims. / **评测标准（2026-09-21）：**作为效果证据的实验一律采用成熟上游套件或社区通用验证集的任务与指标，在冻结留出面板上按匹配预算评分；自编微面板（四例演示、自定义划分、合成簇）属平台验证记录，不作为效果主张展示。
