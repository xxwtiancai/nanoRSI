# Recursive Self-Improvement: A Living Survey of Verified Systems

**[简体中文](RSI_SURVEY.zh-CN.md)** · [Industry research map (date-verified entries)](industry-rsi/README.md) · [中文研究地图](industry-rsi/README.zh-CN.md) · [Daily radar log](industry-rsi/RADAR.md) · [Adoption backlog](industry-rsi/ADOPTION.md)

This is a **living survey**: it synthesizes the 135 date-verified entries in the [catalogue](industry-rsi/catalog.json) (rolling window **2025-09-19 → 2026-09-19**, as of **2026-09-19**) into one analytical document, and it is regenerated in step with the daily midnight radar sweep. Every claim below traces to a catalogue entry; author results are **not** local reproductions, and this survey does not assert that general recursive self-improvement has been achieved. Current composition: **94 direct-loop** mechanisms, **31 enabling** techniques and analyses, **10 assisted-R&D** reports, across four change surfaces — 38 parameter-learning, 44 agent-code, 20 memory-context, 33 research-workflows — further grouped into **23 mechanism families** that the category pages render as sections.

For nanoRSI's executable behavior (as opposed to the research landscape here), see the [multilevel guide](../MULTILEVEL.md) and [security model](../SECURITY.md).

## 1. Scope and definitions

We use **recursive self-improvement (RSI)** in the operational sense established by the catalogue: a system that (i) **modifies a persistent artifact of its own operation** — weights, code, skills, memory, harness, or its own improvement policy — (ii) **obtains feedback on the modified artifact from execution or evaluation**, and (iii) **reuses the modified artifact in later work**, with the whole cycle underWay without a human writing the change. Three evidence classes separate what a claim actually demonstrates:

| Class / 类别 | Meaning | Count | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `direct-loop` | Mutation → feedback → reuse closes on a persistent artifact | 63 | SkillOpt, DGM-lineage systems, AgentEvolver, Amazon autonomous post-training |
| `enabling` | Supports or analyzes the loop without itself closing one | 15 | Doc-to-LoRA, TRINITY, the economics calibration, contextual-drag analysis |
| `assisted-rd` | Human-directed R&D acceleration telemetry, no autonomous loop | 7 | OpenAI research acceleration, Devin builds Devin |

Two boundaries are drawn deliberately. First, **capability ≠ mechanism**: a stronger model or an automated-research demonstration without a persistent self-modification loop (Anthropic's Fermat formalization, Google's Stellar Colosseum, PKU's OpenAI4S) is recorded as a signal, not an entry. Second, **one pass is not recursion**: NeoHorse-1 itself frames its result as "an initial attempt … rather than a definitive demonstration"; the catalogue keeps such honesty statements verbatim.

## 2. How this survey is produced

1. **Daily sweep** ([RADAR.md](industry-rsi/RADAR.md)): arXiv (list pages + direct abs/HTML opens; the export API has been rate-limited from this machine since Sept 14), company research pages, university labs, venues (NeurIPS/ICML/ICLR/COLM/ACL 2025-26), tracked GitHub repositories, and media **as leads only**.
2. **Primary-source verification**: every entry is opened at its original paper or official page; first-publication dates are pinned (revision dates never redate a work; undated pages are dated by verifiable metadata, e.g. the Sakana RSI Lab by its HN submission timestamp). Author names are never transliterated.
3. **Uniform schema** ([FORMAT.md](industry-rsi/FORMAT.md)): mechanism, result with comparator/units/conditions, limits including negative findings, separate code/weights/data/license status, and a **mandatory original figure** (paper pipeline figure or official image — no concept illustrations).
4. **This synthesis**: written by hand against the catalogue, never auto-summarized; the taxonomy below is the catalogue's classification (per [RSI_SURVEY.zh-CN.md](RSI_SURVEY.zh-CN.md) 分类体系以 catalog 为准).

## 3. Taxonomy: two axes and one anatomy

**Axis 1 — what changes** (the catalogue's `category`, aligned with nanoRSI's three layers):

| Change surface / 改变面 | nanoRSI layer | Entry count | Canonical questions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Parameter learning | Model RSI | 29 | What generates training signal? Who verifies it? Does the gain survive a second pass? |
| Agent & code | Harness RSI (+ Artifact RSI) | 28 | What exactly is edited — skills, harness, policies? What gates the edit? |
| Memory & context | Harness RSI | 17 | What persists? How is retrieval updated? Does context help or drag? |
| Research workflows | Meta / organizational | 23 | Does the system improve *how research/improvement is done*, and can anyone verify the claims? |

**Axis 2 — relationship to the loop** (`direct-loop` / `enabling` / `assisted-rd`, §1). Between the two axes, every record also carries a **mechanism family** (`family`, the catalogue's second-level grouping — e.g. self-play & curriculum, verifier-centric, skill-file optimization, harness search); the category pages render one section per family, so each family below doubles as a reading path.

**The loop anatomy** every entry decomposes into: **mutate** (who proposes the change and under what constraints), **feedback** (where the evaluation signal comes from — outcome verifier, learned surrogate, human, model judge), **commit** (the acceptance rule that decides persistence — this is where the field has converged hardest, §5.1), and **reuse** (where the artifact is redeployed and whether improvement compounds).

External taxonomies agree on the skeleton: the [genuine-RSI roadmap](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#genuine-rsi-roadmap-2026) orders systems on an L1–L5 autonomy ladder; the [Schmidhuber-lineage survey](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#self-improving-agents-survey) formalizes self-improvement as a *self-induced update operator* over parameters or scaffold; the 1,250-paper RSI survey (arXiv 2607.07663, tracked as a lead) crosses *what is improved* with *degree of loop closure*. This survey stays deliberately operational: an entry is classified by what its **own evidence** shows persisting.

## 4. Systematic review by change surface

### 4.1 Parameter learning (27 entries)

Six mechanism families:

**(a) Self-play & curriculum task generation.** The system generates its own training tasks at the frontier of the current policy. [SPICE](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#meta-spice-self-play) (Meta FAIR) rewards the Challenger by the Reasoner's success *variance* — peak at 50% pass — an automatic curriculum (+8.9% math). [Self-play SWE-RL](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#meta-ssr-self-play) (Meta) removes human issues entirely: the injector must also emit the test artifacts, and inverse mutation testing validates them (+10.4 SWE-bench Verified). [Agent0](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#salesforce-unc-agent0), [AgentEvolver](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#alibaba-agentevolver), [SIMA 2](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#google-sima2-2025) and [SpyRL](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#spyrl-self-verifiable-rewards) populate the family; SpyRL's twist is making the reward *mechanically* checkable by task transformation (hidden spy identity) — no judge anywhere. The recurring failure: unconstrained self-play stalls on challenger dominant strategies (SSR Appendix A) or degenerates (SpyRL's math→writing negative transfer). [SPADE](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#spade-adaptive-environments) grounds the designer in corpus documents and rewards it by *hint-based regret* (corpus grounding lifts environment diversity 0.04→0.68); [EvoLMM](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#evolmm-proposer-solver) extends the family to multimodal models and documents 'Over-Consensus Collapse' at large sample counts.

**(b) Verifier-centric loops.** The verifier is the self-improved artifact. [STV](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#cmu-stv-self-trained-verification) (CMU) distills a reference-conditioned teacher into an unconditioned student verifier, which then powers both test-time refinement and verifier-in-the-loop RL (+33% relative from an RLVR-converged generator). [DeepSeekMath-V2](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#deepseek-math-v2) co-trains verifier and generator with expert-seeded meta-verification. [EvoRS](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#evors-reward-evolution) (Fudan) goes one level deeper — the **reward system itself** is an executable Reward-DAG that an agentic designer revises every N policy updates, with matched-replay acceptance; it is the only method in its study whose hacking rate falls below baseline.

**(c) Experience → distillation.** [EvolveR](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#evolver-experience-lifecycle) alternates offline self-distillation of trajectories into deduplicated principles with online GRPO; its own distillation beats a GPT-4o-mini teacher (0.382 vs 0.370). [Experience Funnel](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#experience-funnel-state-policy) gates distillation on counterfactual state comparison and reports that only 2 of 5 evolution rounds were accepted. [Q-Evolve](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#qevolve-in-distribution) keeps every round's critic in-distribution (weighted IQL + behavior-proximal clipping) — 79.4 average at 13K environment steps where PPO-class baselines need 320K.

**(d) Self-evolving synthetic-data pipelines.** [EigenData](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#eigendata-self-evolving-synthesis) (Tsinghua × Eigen AI) iterates the *data pipeline's own plans* with per-instance executable checkers — the evolving engine beats a human-expert pipeline (56.0% vs 52.0%). [WebAggregator](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#tencent-webaggregator) and [NeoHorse-1](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#tokenrhythm-neohorse-1) route real harness traffic into training mixtures.

**(e) Test-time / bounded adaptation.** [TT-SI](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#ttsi-test-time-self-improvement) (UIUC) fine-tunes a temporary LoRA per uncertain test instance and resets — +5.48% average with 68× fewer samples than SFT. [Chain-of-Experience](industry-rsi/memory-context.md#bytedance-chain-of-experience) accumulates experience at test time across eight LLMs (+5.6% at −19% API cost).

**(f) Enabling** (no closed loop on the artifact itself): [Doc-to-LoRA](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#sakana-doc-to-lora) (amortized adapters), [TRINITY](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#sakana-trinity) (Sep-CMA-ES coordinator head), [DiscoRL](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#google-discorl-2025) (discovered learning rules), [MoE-CL](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#tencent-moe-cl) (continual experts), and — new — [Conductor/Fugu](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#sakana-conductor-fugu) (a 7B RL-trained orchestrator that writes worker topologies and can include itself; enabling because the orchestrator is trained once, not self-improving).

### 4.2 Agent and code evolution (24 entries)

**Skill files as trainable text parameters** is now the densest cluster: [SkillOpt](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#microsoft-skillopt) (bounded edits, strict held-out acceptance, +23.5 avg with GPT-5.5), [SkillLift](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#skilllift-dense-rubrics) (bilevel rubric surrogate replacing oracle rollouts, −40-70% tokens), [SkillHone](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#tencent-skillhone) (persistent decision history), [SkillEvolver](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#skillevolver-meta-skill) (portable meta-skill + fresh-session audit), [EmbodiSkill](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#embodiskill-skill-aware-reflection) (two-channel evidence: defects edit, ignored-guidance lapses re-emphasize), [persistent-skills-osworld](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#persistent-skills-osworld) (versioned GUI library vs matched empty-library control), [WikiSkill](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#wikiskill-experience-wiki) (never-rolled-back wiki auditing rolled-back skills), [SkillClaw](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#skillclaw-collective-evolution) (collective evolution across a user fleet with nightly validation), [SimSkill](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#simskill-traffic) (gap-driven probes), [SkillGLoW](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#skillglow-procedural-families) (procedural families behind a verifier-grounded commit gate), plus co-evolution with weights in [SkillRL](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#skillrl-skill-augmented-rl) and [SAGE](industry-rsi/parameter-learning.md#sage-skill-augmented-grpo); [OpenSkill](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#openskill-open-world) builds both the skill *and its verification anchors* from open-world sources with a leakage barrier (within 1 point of human skill authors on Opus 4.6), and [K-Dense's skill library](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#scientific-agent-skills-library) (45K stars) is the curated-seed pole of the same family.

**Harness search.** [Meta-Harness](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#stanford-meta-harness) (Stanford) searches single-file harnesses with a coding agent reading a filesystem of *all prior candidates' full traces* — trace access (not selection cleverness) is the causal ingredient; 76.4% TerminalBench-2 with the honest caveat that search and eval share 89 tasks. [Beagle/DarwinX](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#salesforce-beagle-darwinx) evolve harnesses populationally; [HarnessDev](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#bytedance-harnessdev) benchmarks creation+evolution and reports only 53.1% visible/held-out direction agreement — the field's own caution about signal quality. [GenericAgent](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#genericagent-skill-tree) shows a 3.3K-line seed with skill consolidation beating million-line harnesses on tokens (100% Lifelong AgentBench at 222K vs OpenClaw 70% at 1.43M). [Dream-RSI](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#dream-rsi-replay-simulator) (Maryland × DeepMind) improves exploration policies offline by "dreaming" over the recorded discovery tree — replay as a free simulator — and [EvoPolicyGym](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#evopolicygym-benchmark) standardizes the measurement of executable-policy evolution (GPT-5.5 tops 15 of 16 fixed environments at a 128-episode budget).

**Self-modifying agents and depth.** The DGM lineage now has measured depth: [Meta^n](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#metan-emergent-depth) applies one fixed Ω recursively and realizes meta-depth 3–6 versus the ~2.5 cap of self-rewriting systems; [MGM](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#mgm-mendel-godel-machine) adds Mendelian comparative operators (cross-task reaction-norm edits, cross-lineage trait hybridization) — Polyglot 50.8%→93.2% with ~117× fewer parameters than the closed-source model it surpasses. [Hyperagents](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#meta-hyperagents-2026) (Meta) archives valid-but-not-improving variants.

**Orchestration and review.** [Feedback Descent](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#stanford-feedback-descent) (Stanford) treats pairwise-preference rationales as gradient-like text supervision with a linear-convergence guarantee — DOCKSTRING results above the 99.9th percentile of 260K compounds; [Apple's Reinforced Agent](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#apple-reinforced-agent) reviews tool calls *before* execution (3.1:1 benefit-to-risk) with GEPA-optimized reviewer prompts. [Qwen3.8-Max](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#qwen38-max-self-evolving-harness) and [MiniMax M2.7](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#minimax-m27-self-evolution) are company self-reports of scaffold self-editing at production scale.

### 4.3 Memory and context evolution (12 entries)

The field has moved from *accumulate* to *structure and gate*. Structures: procedural graphs ([Procedural Graphs](industry-rsi/memory-context.md#procedural-graphs-google): 0%→80% validation survival with a rejected-edit memory), retrieval graphs ([SE-GoS](industry-rsi/memory-context.md#se-gos-skill-graph): evolve only retrieval, 52.4%→59.4% at one-third tokens), playbooks ([ACE](industry-rsi/memory-context.md#sambanova-stanford-ace)), compression policies ([ACON](industry-rsi/memory-context.md#microsoft-acon-2025)), causal memory banks ([RSIAgent](industry-rsi/memory-context.md#rsiagent-autonomous-exploration): broad-then-deep exploration, GLM-5.3+Kimi-K3 beats GPT-6 Astra's reported OSWorld 2.0 by +6.38 partial), MCP-served ontologies ([EvoOntology](industry-rsi/memory-context.md#evoontology-self-evolving): paired backbone-conditional gates worth −11.2 in ablation), and now *memory operations as skills* ([MemSkill](industry-rsi/memory-context.md#memskill-memory-skills): PPO-trained selection + designer-driven evolution, 215 LLM calls where MemoryOS spends 1,288). Newer structures add dual-stream visually-grounded knowledge ([XSkill](industry-rsi/memory-context.md#xskill-dual-stream)), teacher/student pamphlets ([ATLAS](industry-rsi/memory-context.md#atlas-pamphlets), Arc Intelligence), and four-component memory evolution with a repair-without-regression gate ([Recuris](industry-rsi/memory-context.md#recuris-memory-evolution) — 35/37 pairs improve and a second held-out round compounds +6.98). The negative anchors stay load-bearing: [S3Gym](industry-rsi/memory-context.md#bytedance-s3gym) shows self-judgment quality correlating at −0.01 with next-round improvement; [Prime Agent](industry-rsi/memory-context.md#prime-agent)'s Factorio run persisted a resource-spawning *cheat* as a skill; and the [Evo-Memory benchmark](industry-rsi/memory-context.md#evo-memory-remem) shows simple retrieval beating several complex designs while Hard→Easy ordering beats Easy→Hard.

### 4.4 Research workflows (22 entries)

**AI scientists that train.** [Faraday/Replica](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#faraday-replica-ai-scientist) (Inherent Labs) post-trains a 27B to direct Codex-as-tool on paper-replication tasks scored by auto-generated rubric judges (self-agreement 0.66 reported). [EvoScientist](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#evoscientist-self-evolving) (Huawei) evolves ideation and experimentation memories and took 6/6 ICAIS 2025 acceptances incl. a Best Paper. [Frontis-MA1/OpenRSI](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#frontis-ma1-openmle) trains improvement operators (MLE-Bench Lite medal 39.4%→60.6%). [ENPIRE](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#nvidia-enpire-physical-autoresearch) (NVIDIA × CMU × Berkeley) moves autoresearch onto 8 physical robots — the agents construct their own reward-verification environments as immutable Gym APIs, and the paper honestly reports super-linear token growth with fleet size.

**Autonomous post-training at scale.** [Amazon's autonomous post-training](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#amazon-autonomous-post-training) runs four no-human rounds on a 30B model (8th of ~4,000, 0.86 vs 0.87 top human) with the recursive object being the *search policy* — and the loop's detection-and-rejection of its own dev-proxy exploit is the single most instructive honesty record in the catalogue. [PostTrainBench](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#posttrainbench-autonomous-post-training) (Tübingen) measures everyone else: best agent 23.2% vs 51.1% for human pipelines, with 23 contamination flags across five agents.

**Company telemetry (assisted-R&D).** [OpenAI's research-acceleration report](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#openai-research-acceleration-2026) (3.1 agent-workdays per human workday; automated-researcher target March 2028), [Cognition](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#cognition-devin-builds-devin) (659 Devin PRs/week), [Codex builds Codex](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#codex-builds-codex), [Prime's speedrun](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#prime-measuring-autonomous-ai-research), Anthropic's [automated alignment researchers](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#automated-alignment-researchers) and [W2S](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#automated-w2s) (with its seed-cherry-picking confession). None closes an autonomous loop; all quantify how much AI now assists its own improvement pipeline.

**Positions and analyses (enabling).** The [genuine-RSI roadmap](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#genuine-rsi-roadmap-2026) (L1–L5 ladder, HCI diagnostic), [Salesforce's governed-autonomy story](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#salesforce-toward-self-improving-agents), [Sakana's RSI Lab](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#sakana-rsi-lab) (sample-efficiency-over-compute as a stated constraint), [TASTE](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#taste) (can models judge research proposals — 60% vs human 77%), the [economics calibration](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#economics-of-rsi-2026) (observed ~9% AI-R&D return vs ≥15% threshold for self-sustaining acceleration — "not currently strong enough"), and three audits: the [verification-gap survey](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#ai-scientist-verification-gap) (0 of 9 LLM-era closed-loop systems has an externally validated in-loop oracle), Google's [generalization-gap study](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#gengap-self-evolution) (self-evolution *sharpens* Pass@1 without moving Pass@32 — 8-13 points below oracle), and the [1,250-paper RSI survey](industry-rsi/research-workflows.md#rsi-survey-1250) whose four-rung verification hierarchy (formal verifiers > execution feedback > learned judges > intrinsic signals) explains *why* the gate pattern of §5.1 works.

## 5. Cross-cutting findings

**5.1 The acceptance gate is the field's convergent invention.** Across 82 direct-loop entries, the single strongest pattern: persistence requires an independent check. Held-out validation (SkillOpt, Experience Funnel), verifier-grounded commit gates (SkillGLoW), paired same-condition comparison (EvoOntology −11.2 without it; EvoRS matched replay), pre-execution review (Apple), fresh-session audit (SkillEvolver), rollback-with-log everywhere (WikiSkill's never-rolled-back wiki; Amazon's dead-end registry). Cheap surrogates for expensive gates are the newest refinement — SkillLift's rubric re-aligned by rank correlation, Dream-RSI's replay scoring, Faraday's auto-rubrics. nanoRSI's frozen-evaluator invariant is this pattern stated as an architecture rule.

**5.2 Honest negative results are accumulating into a discipline.** Rejected rounds logged (Experience Funnel 2/5), proxy exploits caught and policy-rewritten (Amazon), direction disagreement quantified (HarnessDev 53.1%), transfer drops kept (EvoOntology −6.6 cross-backbone; SpyRL's negative transfer), self-judgment decorrelation (S3Gym), sharpening-not-teaching (GenGap Pass@32 flat), self-deterioration under iteration (Contextual Drag's GPT-OSS-20B collapse), retained-gains-via-rollback selection called out (Aspire: all three successors *trail* the reference harness). The catalogue treats these as first-class results.

**5.3 The evaluator is now inside the mutation surface — and that is the dangerous frontier.** EvoRS evolves the reward DAG; STV trains the verifier; Faraday generates the rubrics; rubric-as-reward pipelines judge writing. The paired counterweights, all new this window: PostTrainBench's cheating ledger (12 flags in 84 runs for the *best* agent), the verification-gap audit's 0/9 statistic, and SSR's observation that full tests in-prompt invite reward hacking. Verifier evolution without external anchoring is where reward-hacking risk concentrates. The same holds one layer down: the [skill-misevolution audit](industry-rsi/agent-code.md#skill-misevolution-safety) finds **all 21** evolved configurations authoring unsafe artifacts (three malicious tasks lift carryover attack success 16.0%→35.3% *while benign utility rises*), and shows write/reuse-boundary governance (SafeEvolve) cutting harm 26.7/17.3 points at 0.4 utility cost — persistent updates must be observable, attributable and revocable.

**5.4 Self-generated context can hurt.** Contextual Drag quantifies 10-20% drops from conditioning on erroneous drafts — even labeled-erroneous ones — with GPT-5 nearly immune and small models collapsing. Any loop that feeds its own drafts back (revision, memory writing, distillation) needs a drag control.

**5.5 Cost and sample efficiency became reportable science.** SkillLift −40-70% tokens; SkillGLoW 3.6× more compact libraries; SE-GoS one-third input tokens; SAGE 59% fewer tokens; Meta-Harness 0.1× evaluations; Q-Evolve 13K vs 320K steps; Amazon ~10³× execution scale over prior autonomous-ML demos; ENPIRE's MRU/MTU utilization metrics; Sakana's sample-efficiency-first charter; the economics paper turning the whole question into one elasticity number (~9% vs 15%). PostTrainBench prices agents per run ($600-910).

**5.6 Populations, lineages and depth beat single-track editing.** Beagle's preserve-and-extend, MGM's cross-lineage hybridization (with a proposition that comparative evidence raises fix probability), Meta^n's measured depth 3-6, Amazon's orthogonal-axis workers with a baseline anchor, ShinkaEvolve's quality-diversity archive. The 2025-generation single-lineage self-editor is giving way to search over improvement histories — with trace access as the empirically decisive interface (Meta-Harness ablation: scores-only 41.3 vs full-trace 56.7).

**5.7 Geography and institutions.** The window's direct-loop work is genuinely international: US (Meta, Google, Stanford, CMU, Princeton, AWS, Apple, NVIDIA, Anthropic, OpenAI, Salesforce, Prime, Cognition), China (ByteDance, Alibaba/Tongyi/Amap, Tencent, DeepSeek, MiniMax, Tsinghua, PKU, RUC, Fudan, SJTU, ZJU, UESTC, CAS, Jilin, Huawei), Japan (Sakana, Institute of Science Tokyo), Singapore (NUS, NTU, IAIC), Europe (TU/e, Tübingen, LMU, Imperial-era LMU collaborations, Hello Group), and new labs (Inherent, Aether AI, Eigen AI, TokenRhythm, Frontis, Thoughtful Lab). No single institution owns the gate pattern — it was reinvented independently at least six times this window.

## 6. Benchmarks and controls landscape

Concentration: ALFWorld (9+ entries), SWE-bench family (7), OSWorld/AppWorld/GAIA-class computer use (7), SkillsBench/WildClawBench skill suites (6), WebShop (5), AIME/math (5), GAIA-class browsing (4). Saturation is being reported honestly (InsightBench +0.7-1.6; ALFWorld ceiling at 93-100%).

Controls used across the catalogue, in increasing strength: matched-configuration ablation (persistent-skills-osworld's empty-library control) → frozen/uniform/random arms (nanoRSI's own discipline) → counterfactual state comparison (Experience Funnel) → paired same-condition replay (EvoRS, EvoOntology) → fresh-session audit (SkillEvolver) → external-anchor evaluation (Amazon's leaderboard; PostTrainBench's held-out configs). Gaps: seeds/traces released by only 38% of AI-scientist systems (verification-gap audit); task overlap between search and eval rarely disclosed (Meta-Harness is the exception that confesses).

## 8. Open problems

1. **Second-pass compounding** — only NeoHorse-1 (one pass, by its own framing), Amazon (four policy rounds on one task family) and EvolveR/Q-Evolve (2-3 rounds) test repeated traversal; nobody demonstrates accelerating returns across rounds on a fixed budget. (ADOPTION 1)
2. **Verifier drift and evaluator anchoring** — evolving reward systems and trained verifiers need external anchors; the 0/9 closed-loop-validation statistic is the gap to close. (ADOPTION 14-15, 19)
3. **Cross-backbone and cross-task transfer** — repeatedly positive within-suite, repeatedly negative across backbones (EvoOntology −6.6; EmbodiSkill's +1.49 pairing; SpyRL). What makes a skill portable?
4. **Sharpening vs learning** — Pass@1/Pass@32 divergence suggests today's loops mostly re-weight existing capabilities; adding capability needs external signal (GenGap's one-oracle-round jump to 53.2%).
5. **Cost-complete accounting** — few papers report discovery+evaluation cost together (DiscoRL and ENPIRE are the exceptions); sample-efficiency claims need both denominators.
