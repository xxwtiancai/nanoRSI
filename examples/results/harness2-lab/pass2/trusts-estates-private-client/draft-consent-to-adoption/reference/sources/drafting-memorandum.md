## V. Legal and Practical Issues for Attorney Direction

1. **"Immediate irrevocability" request.** Keisha has reportedly stated she wants the consent "final and irrevocable immediately" with no waiting period. Under the Maryland Family Law Article (independent adoption provisions), a consent may be withdrawn within 30 days after signing; a statutory withdrawal right cannot be waived or contracted away. The draft therefore affirmatively discloses the 30-day window. Recommend that you and/or Danica Okafor counsel Keisha directly on this point so her expectations are aligned and any later revocation risk is understood. Any representation to her that the consent is immediately irrevocable would be improper.
2. **Post-adoption contact / visitation understanding.** The parties have an informal understanding that Keisha will see Elijah "a few times a year" (holidays, birthdays). The intake flags the open question whether to include it in the adoption papers. Recommendation: it should **not** be embedded in the consent or petition; any contact arrangement is a separate, private, non-enforceable understanding (Maryland does not enforce open-adoption contact agreements in independent adoptions). The draft includes a paragraph confirming the consent is not conditioned on any contact arrangement. Advise clients accordingly. Note: the unread visitation email may bear directly on this issue.
3. **Execution logistics.** Keisha will sign at our office. Recommend: (a) Ms. Okafor be invited (or at minimum notified) to attend; (b) a notary present; (c) execution in the statutory form; (d) do not obtain Keisha's signature more than the permitted time before filing, if applicable. Target date on or about June 10, 2024.
4. **Father's counsel's statement that the consent "resolves all obligations related to the child."** This is inaccurate as to child support: the Guardianship Order expressly states the existing support order (Case No. 19-FS-0008714, $437/month, arrearages ~$14,421) is not modified or superseded, and arrearages/TANF assignment must be addressed in the appropriate proceeding. The adoption decree will terminate future support obligations prospectively, but accrued arrearages and TANF reimbursement claims survive. This should be clarified to all parties and possibly addressed in the adoption or a support proceeding.
5. **Notice and other required filings.** Confirm what must accompany the consent (e.g., notice to the father, proof of his executed consent, home study filing). The favorable home study (April 12, 2024) should be filed with the court.
6. **Obtain the father's executed consent** from Gerald Tate for the file and court (intake open item 2).
