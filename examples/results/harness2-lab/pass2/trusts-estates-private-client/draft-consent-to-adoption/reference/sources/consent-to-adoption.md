# CONSENT TO ADOPTION BY BIOLOGICAL MOTHER

**IN THE CIRCUIT COURT FOR BALTIMORE CITY**
**STATE OF MARYLAND**

**In re: The Adoption of Elijah James Whitfield, a Minor Child**

**Case No. 24-A-0001537**

---

## I. IDENTIFICATION OF CONSENTING PARENT

I, **Keisha R. Whitfield**, of full age, residing at 1410 East Preston Street, Apt. 3B, Baltimore, Maryland 21213, make the following statement freely and voluntarily, upon my oath and under the penalties of perjury.

1. I was born on September 2, 1993, and I am 31 years of age.
2. I am the biological mother of **Elijah James Whitfield**, a minor child, born on **March 17, 2017** [subject to confirmation — see Drafting Memorandum, Discrepancy No. 1], at Lakeview Regional Medical Center, Baltimore, Maryland, Birth Certificate No. 2017-03-127845, issued by the Maryland Division of Vital Records.
3. I have never been married to the child's biological father, **Darnell Tyrone Whitfield**.

## II. STATEMENT OF UNDERSTANDING

4. I understand that **Marcus Holloway and Diana Holloway (née Whitfield)**, of 2847 Oriole Nest Lane, Towson, Maryland 21204, have filed a Petition for Adoption (Case No. 24-A-0001537) in the Circuit Court for Baltimore City seeking to adopt Elijah. Diana Holloway is Elijah's biological paternal aunt, being the sister of Darnell Tyrone Whitfield.
5. I understand that by signing this Consent I voluntarily and permanently relinquish all of my parental rights to Elijah, including all rights to custody, visitation, and communication with the child, and that the entry of a final decree of adoption will terminate my legal relationship as Elijah's parent, subject to the provisions of the Maryland Family Law Article, Title 5, Subtitle 3.
6. I understand that I am not required to sign this Consent, and that I have the right to consult with independent legal counsel. I am represented by Danica Okafor, Esq., of Okafor Legal Services LLC, 305 East Fayette Street, Suite 200, Baltimore, Maryland 21202, and I have consulted with her regarding this Consent and its consequences.
7. I understand that I may withdraw this Consent **at any time within 30 days after signing it**, as provided by Md. Code, Family Law Article, § 5-349, and that after that period the Consent becomes irrevocable except upon a finding of fraud or duress. **No provision of this Consent can eliminate or shorten this statutory 30-day withdrawal period, notwithstanding any expressed preference for immediate irrevocability** (see Drafting Memorandum, Issue No. 1).
8. I understand that no person may accept or receive a payment or anything of value in exchange for this Consent, and that no compensation or financial inducement has been promised or given to me in exchange for it. Md. Code, Family Law Article, § 5-335 et seq.
9. I understand that a Temporary Guardianship Order was entered on August 3, 2023 (Case No. 23-G-0004218), granting temporary guardianship of Elijah to Marcus and Diana Holloway, and that Elijah has resided with them continuously since that date.
10. I understand that any understanding regarding future contact or visitation between me and Elijah is a private, non-binding arrangement that is not enforceable as part of the adoption or of this Consent, and that this Consent is not conditioned upon any such contact (see Drafting Memorandum, Issue No. 2).

## III. VOLUNTARY CONSENT

11. I hereby consent freely and voluntarily to the adoption of my son, Elijah James Whitfield, by Marcus Holloway and Diana Holloway, and I consent to the entry of a decree of adoption in Case No. 24-A-0001537 in the Circuit Court for Baltimore City.
12. I represent that this Consent is made knowingly and voluntarily, free of fraud, duress, or undue influence, and that I have received no payment or other consideration for giving it.

## IV. ACKNOWLEDGMENT AND OATH

I declare under the penalties of perjury that the foregoing statements are true and correct to the best of my knowledge, information, and belief.
