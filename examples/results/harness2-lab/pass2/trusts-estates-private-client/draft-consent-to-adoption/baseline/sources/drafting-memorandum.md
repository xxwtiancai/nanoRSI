## 4. Open Legal Issues Requiring Direction

1. **Rescission period.** As above — the mother's stated wish cannot be honored; consider explaining at execution with her counsel present.
2. **Openness/visitation.** The parties have an informal understanding of holiday/birthday visits. Any post-adoption contact agreement (per Fam. Law § 5-543/§ 5-549 framework) should be a **separate, court-approved agreement — not embedded in the consent**, because the consent must be unconditional. Guardianship Order ¶ 3 currently preserves the mother's "reasonable contact," which will terminate upon adoption. Recommend a separate post-adoption contact agreement if the parties wish contact to be enforceable.
3. **Execution logistics.** Keisha will sign at our office; recommend her counsel (Danica Okafor) be present and sign the certificate of independent counsel to bolster voluntariness, and that the consent be executed before a judge or notary per statute.
4. **Father's consent.** Obtain and review the executed March 1, 2024 Consent and Relinquishment from Gerald Tate to confirm statutory compliance (witnessed/notarized at Jessup); confirm no separate termination proceeding is required.
5. **Notice to DHR/TANF.** Given TANF receipt (Oct. 2021–June 2023), confirm notice to the Maryland Department of Human Services and address any assignment of support rights.
6. **Birth certificate.** Obtain certified copy to resolve the DOB discrepancy (Item 3.1) before finalizing the consent.

## 5. Recommendations

Proceed with the attached draft consent (with the statutory rescission notice), resolve the DOB discrepancy against the certified birth certificate, review the three unopened email files, secure the father's executed consent for the file, and address visitation through a separate written agreement rather than the consent instrument.
