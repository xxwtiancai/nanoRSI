## CONSENT OF BIOLOGICAL MOTHER TO ADOPTION

I, **Keisha R. Whitfield**, being first duly sworn, depose and state as follows:

### 1. Identity of Consenting Parent

1.1. I am the biological mother of **Elijah James Whitfield**, a minor child, whose date of birth is recorded on his birth certificate as **March 17** [**NOTE: YEAR DISCREPANCY — see Drafting Memorandum; intake records state 2018, the Temporary Guardianship Order (Case No. 23-G-0004218) and Home Study Report state 2017. 1.2. I reside at 1410 East Preston Street, Apt. 3B, Baltimore, Maryland 21213. My date of birth is September 2, 1993.

1.3. I have never been married to the child's biological father, **Darnell Tyrone Whitfield**, who is named as the father on the child's birth certificate and who executed a Consent and Relinquishment of Parental Rights on March 1, 2024.

### 2. Identity of Prospective Adoptive Parents

2.1. I hereby consent to the adoption of my son, Elijah James Whitfield, by **Marcus Holloway** and **Diana Holloway (née Whitfield)**, husband and wife, residing at 2847 Oriole Nest Lane, Towson, Maryland 21204. Diana Holloway is my son's biological paternal aunt.

### 3. Voluntariness of Consent

3.1. I make this consent freely and voluntarily, without coercion, duress, fraud, or undue influence of any kind.

3.2. No payment, compensation, or other thing of value has been offered or given to me by the prospective adoptive parents, or by anyone acting on their behalf, in exchange for this consent.

3.3. I have been advised of my right to be represented by independent counsel in this matter, and I am represented by **Danica Okafor, Esq.**, of Okafor Legal Services LLC, 305 East Fayette Street, Suite 200, Baltimore, Maryland 21202. I have consulted with my counsel regarding this consent, or have been offered the opportunity to do so before signing.

3.4. I understand that I may have this consent reviewed by an attorney of my own choosing prior to execution, and that I may take up to ten (10) days after the petition for adoption is filed to review the consent, as provided by Md. Code Ann., Fam. Law § 5-335.

### 4. Understanding of Legal Effect

4.1. I understand that by signing this consent, I permanently and irrevocably relinquish all parental rights to Elijah James Whitfield, including the right to custody, visitation, and decision-making concerning the child.

4.2. I understand that upon entry of a final decree of adoption, I will have no legal relationship with the child and no legal right to contact or visitation with the child, and that any post-adoption contact will be solely at the discretion of the adoptive parents unless otherwise ordered by a court of competent jurisdiction.

4.3. I understand that this consent may be used as a basis for the termination of my parental rights and for the entry of an adoption decree without further notice to me.

### 5. Right of Rescission (Statutory Notice)

5.1. **NOTICE REQUIRED BY LAW.** I understand that, notwithstanding my expressed personal wish that this consent be final and irrevocable immediately, under Maryland law I have the right to rescind this consent by written notice delivered to the prospective adoptive parents or their attorney within thirty (30) days after I sign this consent, or before the entry of an interlocutory or final decree of adoption, whichever occurs first. I understand that this statutory right cannot be waived by any provision of this document.

### 6. Statements Regarding Circumstances

6.1. I acknowledge that the child has resided with Marcus and Diana Holloway since August 3, 2023, pursuant to a Temporary Guardianship Order entered by the Circuit Court for Baltimore City (Case No. 23-G-0004218).

6.2. I acknowledge that I completed inpatient treatment at Harborview Recovery Center on January 15, 2024, and am engaged in continuing outpatient counseling, and that my decision to consent is made knowingly and free of impairment.
