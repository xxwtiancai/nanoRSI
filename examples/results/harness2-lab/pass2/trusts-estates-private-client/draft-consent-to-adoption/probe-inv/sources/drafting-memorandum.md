## 3. Drafting Decisions Requiring Attorney Direction

1. **Irrevocability on signing — cannot be drafted as requested.** Clients relayed Keisha's wish that the consent be "final and irrevocable immediately upon signing" with no waiting period. Maryland's independent adoption statutes provide judicial/agency-acknowledgment and limited revocation protections that cannot be waived by private agreement. The draft states the statutory protections in ¶ 4.3 and flags the point for explanation to Ms. Whitfield at execution. Counsel should confirm she understands the consent is *not* immediately irrevocable notwithstanding her stated preference.
2. **Post-adoption contact.** The informal understanding (occasional visits on holidays/birthdays) is acknowledged in ¶ 4.5 as non-enforceable and expressly not a condition of consent. The intake memo flags as unresolved whether to formalize a post-adoption contact agreement; that decision remains open. Note Temporary Guardianship Order ¶ 3 granted Keisha "reasonable contact" rights — that provision is effectively superseded on adoption but should be addressed at the hearing.
3. **Execution logistics.** The draft contemplates execution before the assigned judge, a master, or an authorized agency representative rather than simple notarization; confirm the correct method under FL § 5-345. Clients asked whether Danica Okafor must attend — her attendance is strongly recommended (or her written confirmation of advice) to defend against later challenges to voluntariness. Intake target date for execution: on or about June 10, 2024.
4. **No inducement.** Petitioners represent no compensation was provided; the non-inducement acknowledgment is included in ¶ 4.1.
