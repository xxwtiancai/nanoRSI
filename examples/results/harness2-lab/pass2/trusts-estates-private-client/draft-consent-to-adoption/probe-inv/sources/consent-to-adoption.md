## 3. Statement of Consent

3.1. ## 4. Acknowledgments

4.1. **Voluntariness.** I am signing this consent freely and voluntarily, of my own will, without coercion, duress, threat, force, fraud, or undue influence of any kind. No person has offered or given me any payment, compensation, reward, or other thing of value, and no person has promised me any such inducement, in exchange for giving this consent.

4.2. **Independent Counsel.** I have been represented by independent counsel of my own choosing, **Danica Okafor, Esq., Okafor Legal Services LLC, 305 East Fayette Street, Suite 200, Baltimore, Maryland 21202**, in connection with my decision to consent to this adoption. I have had the opportunity to consult with my attorney and to have this document explained to me by my attorney before signing.

4.3. **Understanding of Consequences.** I understand the nature and effect of this consent and of the adoption proceeding, including that the consent, once given in the manner required by Maryland law and acknowledged before a representative of a licensed child placement agency or the court, is intended to be final and, except as limited by Maryland law, may not be revoked except for good cause shown, including fraud or duress, before the entry of the final decree of adoption. I understand that I have the right to withdraw this consent within any period provided by Maryland law and that after the statutory revocation period the consent becomes irrevocable except upon a showing of fraud or duress.

[NOTE TO SUPERVISING ATTORNEY: The clients relayed that Ms. Whitfield wishes the consent to be "final and irrevocable immediately upon signing" with no waiting or revocation period. Maryland law (Fam. Law § 5-345 et seq.) does not permit a natural mother to waive the statutory revocation/court-approval protections by private agreement. The consent is drafted with the statutory protections stated; the client-relayed wish cannot be implemented as requested. This point must be explained to Ms. Whitfield at execution and confirmed with her counsel.]

4.4. **Medical and Social History.** I have truthfully provided such medical and social history information regarding myself and the child's father as has been requested of me, and I authorize release of such information to the adoptive parents and the court.

4.5. **Contact Understanding.** I understand that the adoptive parents and I have an informal, good-faith understanding that I may have limited future contact with the child, such as occasional visits around holidays and birthdays. I understand that any such contact is **not enforceable and does not affect the validity of this consent**, and that the adoptive parents will have sole authority to decide whether any contact occurs following the adoption. [NOTE TO SUPERVISING ATTORNEY: The parties have asked whether this understanding should be formalized in a post-adoption contact agreement; this remains an open item for attorney direction and is not made a term of this consent as drafted.]
