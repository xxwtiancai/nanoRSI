## III. CONSENT

13. I hereby consent freely, knowingly, and voluntarily to the adoption of my son, **Elijah James Whitfield**, by **Marcus Holloway and Diana Holloway (née Whitfield)**, and I consent to the entry of a decree of adoption in the above-captioned case.
