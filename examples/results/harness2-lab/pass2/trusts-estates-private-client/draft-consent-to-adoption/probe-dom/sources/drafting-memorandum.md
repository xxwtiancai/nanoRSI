## IV. OPEN LEGAL ISSUES FOR ATTORNEY DECISION

**1. Immediate irrevocability requested by Keisha.** Keisha reportedly wants the consent "final and irrevocable immediately" with no waiting period. Maryland law (FL § 5-3B-07) provides a limited statutory window to challenge or rescind a consent (30 days from execution or entry of an interloculatory decree, whichever occurs first). We cannot draft around the statute. The draft therefore includes the statutory language and an acknowledgment that her personal wish cannot override it. Recommend Attorney Chen and/or Ms. Okafor counsel Keisha on this point and document the advice.

**2. Presence of Keisha's counsel at execution.** The clients asked whether Danica Okafor must attend the signing. Strongly recommend she be invited; coordinate execution for on or about June 10, 2024 at our offices.

**3. Birth-parent expenses.** The clients represent no compensation or inducement was paid. Confirm in writing whether any permitted birth-parent expenses were advanced; if so, they must be disclosed and court-approved.

**4. Open adoption / visitation understanding.** The parties have an informal understanding that Keisha will visit a few times a year (holidays/birthdays). The Temporary Guardianship Order currently preserves Keisha's right to reasonable contact; that order will be superseded by the adoption. Any post-adoption contact arrangement must be handled through a separate, court-approved written agreement as Maryland permits — it should **not** be embedded in the consent as a condition — and Keisha should be advised that an informal understanding is unenforceable. Attorney decision needed on whether to draft a post-adoption contact agreement. (The unread client-visitation-email.eml likely bears directly on this and must be reviewed.)

**5. Statutory form/citation confirmation.** The draft uses a general FL § 5-3B-06/07 framework. Confirm current statutory citations, local Baltimore City form requirements, and whether the court prefers execution before a judge or master rather than a notary.

**6. Father's executed consent.** Obtain the original March 1, 2024 Consent and Relinquishment of Parental Rights from Gerald Tate and verify execution, notarization, and compliance with statutory requirements for an incarcerated signatory; the unread father-counsel-letter.eml may contain representations requiring verification.
