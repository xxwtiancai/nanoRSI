## I. Executive Summary

This memorandum reviews the draft Form 8-K (dated July 7, 2025; signature block dated July 8, 2025) against the deal documents (APA summary, SPA summary, Amendment No. 2 credit facility term sheet, Board resolutions, press release) and the internal filing checklist. The draft covers Items 1.01, 5.01, 7.01 and 9.01, but contains **material omissions and mischaracterizations requiring correction before filing**, including a **four-business-day deadline issue** (the earliest event reported — Brennan's resignation notice of July 5, 2025 — triggers a July 11, 2025 filing deadline under Item 5.02, if that item is properly characterized).

### Critical Issues (Action Required)

1. **Wrong item number used for CFO transition.** The draft uses Item 5.01 (Changes in Control of Registrant). The checklist confirms **no change in control occurred**; the CFO departure/appointment is an **Item 5.02** matter. This is a substantive mischaracterization.
2. **Item 2.01 (Completion of Acquisition) is omitted** as a separately captioned item, even though the acquisition closed on July 7, 2025. The checklist treats Item 2.01 as triggered (combined with Item 1.01 permissible, but the item should be captioned).
3. **Item 2.03 (Direct Financial Obligation) is omitted.** The $15,000,000 draw and the increase of the revolving commitment from $30,000,000 to $50,000,000 under Amendment No. 2 clearly trigger Item 2.03. The draft addresses the credit facility only in passing under "Funding of the Acquisition."
4. **Item 3.02 (Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities) is omitted.** Three unregistered issuances occurred: (i) 562,500 PIPE shares at $16.00/share ($9,000,000); (ii) 1,562,500 shares issued to Ridgeline at a deemed $8.00/share ($12,500,000); and (iii) Placement Agent Warrants to Calloway Marsh Capital LLC for 28,125 shares at $16.00/share. The warrants are disclosed **nowhere** in the draft.
5. **Factual errors in Item 1.01**: stock consideration misstated as **1,500,000 shares** (correct: **1,562,500 shares**); Treyvino's reporting line misstated as CEO (correct per APA summary: **CTO**); data center location misstated as Raleigh, NC (correct per APA summary: **Boulder, CO** facility); customer contracts misstated as "approximately 78 active contracts" and patents detail not corroborated by deal documents — verify against executed APA schedules; Amendment No. 1 date misstated as **June 30, 2024** (correct per term sheet and Board resolutions: **March 3, 2024**); leverage covenant description inconsistent with term sheet (see Section III below); survival of IP reps (24 months) not corroborated by the APA summary.
6. **Item 9.01(a)/(b):** Draft states "Not applicable" for financial statements of the business acquired and pro forma financial information without a Rule 3-05 significance analysis. Significance testing (investment, asset, and income tests) must be completed; if any test exceeds 20%, Ridgeline audited financials and Article 11 pro formas are required (may be filed by amendment within 71 calendar days, i.e., by approximately September 16, 2025).
7. **Exhibit index errors:** Exhibit 10.3 mislabeled as Lindqvist "Employment Agreement"; the **Brennan Separation Agreement** is missing; the **Form of Registration Rights Agreement** and **Form of Placement Agent Warrant** exhibits are missing (per checklist, Exhibits 10.4 and 10.5).
8. **Cover page share count** states 42,500,000 shares outstanding as of July 1, 2025. While the as-of date may technically predate closing, consider updating or supplementing to reflect the post-transaction count of 44,625,000 shares (42,500,000 + 1,562,500 + 562,500; warrants excluded).

## III. Item-by-Item Analysis

### Item 1.01 — Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement (Present; corrections needed)

**APA disclosure — generally thorough, but contains the following discrepancies against the APA summary and Board resolutions:**

1. **Stock Consideration share count:** Draft states 1,500,000 shares representing ~$12,500,000 at $8.00/share. The math and all source documents (APA summary §3.2; Board resolutions §II; press release) confirm **1,562,500 shares** ($12,500,000 ÷ $8.00). **Correct.**
2. **Acquired asset detail:** Draft describes "approximately 78 active customer contracts," "three issued U.S. patents and two pending applications," and a "data center facility in Raleigh, North Carolina." The APA summary identifies the tangible equipment as located at Ridgeline's **Boulder, CO** facility (Schedule 2.1) and does not corroborate the contract count, patent counts, or any Raleigh facility. **Verify each figure against the executed APA schedules or delete.**
3. **Trevino reporting line:** Draft states Mr. Trevino reports to the **CEO**; the APA summary states he reports to the **Chief Technology Officer**. **Correct.**
4. **Survival of representations:** Draft states 18 months general / 36 months fundamental / **24 months for IP representations**. The APA summary provides only 18 months general and 36 months fundamental; the 24-month IP tier is not corroborated. **Verify against executed APA §5 or delete.**
5. **Indemnification:** Draft's basket ($475,000 / ~1%) and cap ($14,250,000 / 30%) are not stated in the APA summary. **Verify against executed APA.**
6. **Earnout payment mechanics:** Draft states payment within 30 business days of milestone achievement; the APA summary provides for certification within 30 days and payment within 30 days thereafter. Also, the draft describes the milestones as sequential in tone; the APA summary confirms the milestones are **independently measured** (Milestone 2 achievable without Milestone 1). **Align description.**
7. **Forward-looking synergy statements:** The paragraph anticipating revenue synergies and ARR contribution within Item 1.01 is unusual for an Item 1.01 entry into agreement disclosure; consider moving to the press release only (Item 7.01) to avoid conflating filed statements with furnished statements.
8. **Amendment No. 2 description (under "Funding of the Acquisition"):**
   - Draft misdates Amendment No. 1 as **June 30, 2024**; term sheet and Board resolutions confirm **March 3, 2024**. **Correct.**
   - Draft states leverage covenant increased from 3.00:1.00 to 3.50:1.00 for four quarters, stepping down; the term sheet shows the **3.50:1.00 maximum net leverage ratio is unchanged** and the **new** covenant added by Amendment No. 2 is the **$10,000,000 minimum liquidity covenant** (not mentioned in draft). **Correct.**
   - Draft omits the interest rate increase (from SOFR + 2.25% to SOFR + 2.75%), the $200,000 aggregate amendment/upfront fees, and the 30-day post-closing supplemental security agreement over the acquired OrchestraAI IP. Include or cross-reference the new Item 2.03 section.
   - Draft's note that private placement raised "$9,000,000 in proceeds" should acknowledge **gross** proceeds; net proceeds were approximately $8,475,000 after the $450,000 placement agent fee and ~$75,000 expenses (per SPA summary). Clarify.
9. **SPA treatment:** The SPA (executed July 3, 2025) is itself a material definitive agreement but is described only as a funding source. Either expand Item 1.01 treatment (parties, 562,500 shares at $16.00, closing conditioned on concurrent APA closing, 6-month lock-up, registration rights, indemnification cap $2,250,000 / basket $112,500) or confirm with outside counsel that filing the SPA as Exhibit 10.2 with Item 3.02 disclosure is sufficient.

**Bottom line (Item 1.01): Revise for the factual corrections above; add caption for Item 2.01 cross-reference.**

### Item 2.03 — Creation of a Direct Financial Obligation (MISSING — REQUIRED)

The $15,000,000 draw on July 7, 2025 and the $20,000,000 commitment increase trigger Item 2.03. Per the checklist and term sheet, the new section must disclose:

- Commitment increase: $30,000,000 → $50,000,000; initial draw of $15,000,000.
- Interest: Term SOFR (with 0.10% credit spread adjustment) + 2.75% (increased from + 2.25%); default rate + 2.00%.
- Maturity: September 15, 2027 (unchanged).
- Financial covenants: maximum net leverage ratio 3.50:1.00 (quarterly); **new minimum liquidity covenant of $10,000,000** (at all times; quarterly and spot testing); minimum consolidated EBITDA $8,000,000 (trailing four quarters).
- Negative covenants: additional indebtedness >$2,000,000, liens, dispositions >$1,000,000/year, dividends upon default, acquisitions >$5,000,000 without consent (consent obtained for Ridgeline), change of control.
- Events of default: payment, financial covenant (no cure), covenant (30-day cure, with carve-outs), misrepresentation, cross-default >$2,000,000, bankruptcy, change of control (>35% beneficial ownership or delisting), judgments >$3,000,000, ERISA events >$1,000,000.

- Collateral: first-priority security interest in substantially all assets; 30-day post-closing supplemental security agreement over acquired OrchestraAI IP.
- File Amendment No. 2 as Exhibit 10.1 (already listed).
