## ITEM-BY-ITEM ANALYSIS

### Item 1.01 — Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement

**Status: Substantially covered, with corrections needed.**

Correctly triggered and reported. Verified against sources:

- Purchase price of up to $47,500,000 = $28,000,000 cash + $12,500,000 stock + up to $7,000,000 earnout — **confirmed** (APA summary §3; board resolutions §II; press release).
- Earnout milestones: $3,500,000 at $15,000,000 ARR within 12 months; $3,500,000 at $25,000,000 ARR within 24 months — **confirmed**, subject to corrections:
  - Draft says payment is due "within 30 business days following" ARR achievement. APA summary §3.3 provides payment within **30 days following delivery of a milestone achievement certification**, with certification due within 30 days of the measurement date. Conform the mechanics.
  - APA summary states each milestone is **independently measured** (Milestone 2 can be earned without Milestone 1); the draft's framing should be conformed.
- Assumed liabilities: $2,300,000 trade payables + $1,100,000 deferred revenue = $3,400,000 — **confirmed** (APA summary §4; board resolutions; press release).
- Indemnification basket of $475,000 (~1%) and cap of $14,250,000 (30%) — not corroborated in the APA summary (truncated section); verify against executed APA before filing.
- **Defect:** Stock consideration stated as **1,500,000 shares**; correct figure per all sources is **1,562,500 shares**.
- **Defect:** Draft references equipment at a "Raleigh, North Carolina" data center; APA summary Schedule 2.1 assets are at the **Boulder, CO** facility. Correct or verify.
- **Defect:** Survival periods — draft adds a 24-month IP survival tier not supported by the APA summary (18/36 months only). Verify.
- **Defect:** Trevino's reporting line — draft says CEO; APA summary says **CTO**. Correct.
- Missing Item 1.01 element: a description of the **Securities Purchase Agreement** as a material definitive agreement (SPA dated July 3, 2025; three accredited purchasers; 562,500 shares at $16.00; Section 4(a)(2)/Rule 506(b) exemption; registration rights) — see also Item 3.02 below.
- Forward-looking synergy language ("over 350 enterprise customers," ARR contribution expectations) appears in the filed Item 1.01 text without cautionary language; consider moving to furnished Item 7.01/press release or adding safe-harbor language.

### Item 2.03 — Creation of a Direct Financial Obligation

**Status: MISSING — ACTION REQUIRED (Critical).**

The checklist flags this as a required item. The draft addresses the credit facility only within the Item 1.01 "Funding of the Acquisition" paragraph. A standalone Item 2.03 section must be added disclosing (per checklist and credit term sheet):

1. **Obligation:** $15,000,000 drawn July 7, 2025 under the amended revolving facility; revolving commitment increased from $30,000,000 to $50,000,000 ($20,000,000 increase) effective July 7, 2025.
2. **Material terms:** interest at **Term SOFR + 2.75%** (increased from SOFR + 2.25%; draft omits the prior rate and the 0.10% credit spread adjustment — include per term sheet §2.3); maturity **September 15, 2027** (unchanged); first-priority security interest in substantially all assets; **supplemental security agreement required within 30 days** covering acquired Ridgeline assets including OrchestraAI IP (post-closing covenant omitted from draft).
3. **Fees:** amendment fee $100,000 and upfront fee on the incremental commitment $100,000 (total $200,000); unused line fee 0.25% per annum — omitted from draft.
4. **Financial covenants:** minimum liquidity of $10,000,000 (new covenant, at all times, quarterly and spot testing); maximum net leverage ratio 3.50:1.00; minimum consolidated EBITDA $8,000,000.
   - **Defect:** Draft's Item 1.01 text states Amendment No. 2 raised leverage from 3.00:1.00 to 3.50:1.00 with a step-down. Term sheet §3 and board resolutions §V state the 3.50:1.00 covenant is **unchanged**. Resolve conflict against the executed Amendment and correct.
5. **Events of default:** no cure period for financial covenants; cross-default threshold $2,000,000; change-of-control event at 35% beneficial ownership; judgment threshold $3,000,000.
6. **Prior credit history:** original agreement dated September 15, 2023; Amendment No. 1 dated **March 3, 2024** (draft incorrectly says June 30, 2024).

Exhibit 10.1 ties to this item and is properly listed, but the related disclosure section must be added.
