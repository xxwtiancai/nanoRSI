## 1. Executive Summary

Cascade Robotics, Inc. (the "Company") proposes to issue approximately 5,500,000 shares of Series C Preferred Stock at $8.18 per share ($45,000,000 aggregate; $180,000,000 pre-money / $225,000,000 post-money) to Ridgeline Growth Partners Fund VI, L.P. ($35,000,000) and existing preferred holders exercising pro rata participation rights ($10,000,000).

**Key conclusions:**

1. **The pre-money fully diluted share count of 22,004,000 used in the Series C term sheet is not supported by the Company's own records.** The Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation (filed 8/12/2022) reflects **3,820,616** shares of Series A Preferred outstanding (including 320,616 shares issued on conversion of the Firstvale convertible note), and the Company's own cap table shows an available option pool of 1,275,000 and a 150,000-share warrant. Depending on which figures govern, the pre-money fully diluted count could be materially higher than 22,004,000, which would **reduce the price per share below $8.18** and increase the shares issued.
2. **Term sheet § 1.10 requires a pre-closing option pool increase of 2,500,000 shares (5,000,000 → 7,500,000) to be included in the pre-money fully diluted capitalization, but § 1.6's stated 22,004,000-share denominator does not include the increase.** If § 1.10 is honored, the denominator would be at least 24,504,000 shares (computed), yielding a price of approximately $7.34 per share and approximately 6,126,000 Series C shares. **This internal contradiction must be reconciled before signing definitive documents.**
3. The Series C terms deviate materially from the Series A/B precedent in the investor's favor: **1.5x non-participating liquidation preference (vs. 1x)**, **full ratchet anti-dilution (vs. broad-based weighted average)**, seniority over Series A/B in the waterfall, a 2x IPO price threshold for automatic conversion (vs. 3x for Series A), and highly expansive protective provisions (including Series C consent over *all* equity plan issuances and C-level hiring/compensation).
4. Several factual discrepancies exist across the documents (warrant date/exercise price, board designee names, founder name, option pool availability) that must be reconciled because cap table accuracy is a closing condition under term sheet §§ 1.6 and 5.6.

## 6. 409A Considerations

The 9/30/2024 409A valuation sets common FMV at **$8.75/share**, above the proposed Series C preferred price of $8.18. The 409A report (dated 10/18/2024) states no term sheet had been signed as of the valuation date and identified no subsequent events; the term sheet is dated 11/8/2024. The signed term sheet is a material event for 409A purposes; the Company should assess whether a new 409A valuation is required before further option grants (including from the increased pool). Existing grants at $0.50–$6.50 are unaffected. Flag for compensation/tax counsel.

## 8. Conclusions and Recommendations

1. **Do not treat 22,004,000 as the operative pre-money denominator.** The charter-governed Series A count (3,820,616), the exercised-option common (450,000), the warrant (150,000), the true pool (1,275,000), and the § 1.10 pool increase (2,500,000) must all be resolved; on a fully corrected basis the denominator is approximately 25,645,616 and the price approximately $7.02/share (computed).
2. **Reconcile the term sheet's § 1.6 / § 1.10 contradiction in writing** before definitive documents; as drafted, price and share count cannot both be correct.
3. **Negotiate the non-precedent Series C terms**: 1.5x preference → 1x; full ratchet → broad-based weighted average (matching Series A/B); strike § 2.5(b) EIP-consent overreach and § 2.5(g), (h), and (k) (debt, budget, executive hiring/compensation) or raise thresholds; consider the 3x IPO conversion trigger per Series A precedent.
4. **Require Ridgeline joinders** to the Voting Agreement (Exhibit C joinder, § 4.1) and the IRA at closing; Board expansion beyond five seats requires Voting Agreement amendment and consents under its § 2.1.
5. **Condition closing** on a transfer-agent-certified, reconciled cap table and a refreshed 409A valuation.
