## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Cascade Robotics, Inc. (Delaware) proposes a $45,000,000 Series C financing at a $180,000,000 pre-money / $225,000,000 post-money valuation, led by Ridgeline Growth Partners Fund VI, L.P. ($35,000,000), with $10,000,000 of pro rata participation by existing preferred holders. The term sheet sets a price of $8.18 per share based on a stated pre-money fully diluted capitalization of 22,004,000 shares, yielding approximately 5,500,000 Series C shares.

Our review of the eight deal documents identified **material discrepancies that directly affect the deal math and closing conditions**, most significantly: (a) the fully diluted share count underlying the $8.18 price omits 450,000 shares of exercised-option common stock, the 150,000-share Pinnacle warrant, and 320,616 Series A shares issued on conversion of the Firstvale convertible note; (b) the term sheet is internally inconsistent as to whether the 2,500,000-share option pool increase is in the pre-money denominator; and (c) the option pool "available" figure conflicts (1,275,000 vs. 1,204,000). Because the Company represents the cap table's accuracy as a **condition to closing** (Term Sheet §§ 1.6, 1.11, 5.6), these items must be reconciled before signing. On a corrected fully diluted basis (22,675,000–23,195,616 shares), the $180,000,000 pre-money implies a price of **$7.76–$7.94 per share** (computed), not $8.18.

## 6. DISCREPANCIES AND RECOMMENDED ACTIONS

1. **Term sheet internal conflict on pool increase (§1.6 vs. §1.10).** §1.10 requires the 2019 EIP reserve to increase from 5,000,000 to 7,500,000 pre-closing and to be "included in the pre-money Fully Diluted Capitalization," yet §1.6 fixes the denominator at 22,004,000, which does not include the 2,500,000 increase. **Impact:** ~10% price difference; affects every share count and all pro forma percentages. **Action:** negotiate a single defined denominator; if the pool increase is genuinely pre-money, price should be recomputed (~$7.15 on a corrected base).
2. **Omitted securities in the 22,004,000 denominator.** 450,000 exercised-option common shares and the 150,000 Pinnacle warrant are excluded, contrary to §1.5's stated inclusion of "all outstanding" shares and warrants and to the cap table's own tabs. **Impact:** founders'/employees' diluted ownership is understated; price overstated. **Action:** correct the buildup; expect price renegotiation to ~$7.94 (or $7.76).
3. **Series A share count: 3,500,000 (cap table) vs. 3,820,616 (COI §4.2(b), §5A.1).** The charter — the primary source — states 320,616 Series A shares were issued on conversion of the Firstvale Note ($769,479.45 at $2.40). The cap table footnote says the note "converted at Series A closing" but omits the shares. **Action:** confirm with the transfer agent (Ashford Financial Services) whether certificate SA-001 covers 3,820,616 or whether a second certificate exists; reconcile the ledger to the charter. This also changes Firstvale's pro rata participation amount.
4. **Available pool: 1,275,000 (Option Grants tab; 5,000,000 − 3,725,000) vs. 1,204,000 (Summary buildup; Term Sheet §1.6(v)).** Difference of 71,000 is unexplained. **Action:** reconcile; the larger, arithmetic-verified figure (1,275,000) appears correct.
5. **Warrant terms conflict.** Cap table/409A: Pinnacle warrant dated 6/22/2021, $3.00 exercise. COI §4.2(e): warrant dated 3/10/2022, $3.50 exercise. **Action:** obtain the executed warrant; conform the charter and cap table.
6. **Options outstanding: 3,100,000 (cap table) vs. "approximately 2,800,000" (COI §4.2(d)).** The COI figure is as of 8/12/2022 and may pre-date later grants; the cap table grant-level schedule (OPT-001 through OPT-020) reconciles internally to 3,100,000. **Action:** treat cap table as current; add a bring-down note to the COI recital.
7. **Founder name: "Raj Venkatesh" (cap table, 409A, IRA Key Employee definition) vs. "Raj Subramanian" (Note Purchase Agreement recitals; Voting Agreement Key Holder definition).** **Action:** confirm legal name; conform all documents before closing (affects signature blocks and reps).
8. **Board designee names.** Cap table Series A note lists "Thomas Aldridge" and Series B note lists "Victoria Langford"; the IRA, Voting Agreement, and 409A report uniformly identify **Allison Trang** (Series A) and **Derek Hollowell** (Series B). **Action:** correct the cap table notes.
9. **409A common FMV ($8.75) exceeds the Series C preferred price ($8.18).** A common-stock 409A value above the price being paid for senior preferred stock is economically anomalous and may signal (a) an over-optimistic 409A (the 409A predates the term sheet and assumed total equity value of ~$145M vs. the $180M pre-money), or (b) an underpriced Series C. **Impact:** option-pricing/grant compliance (Plan §4.2 requires exercise price ≥ FMV) and diligence optics. **Action:** flag for the Board; consider a refreshed 409A after the Series C prices.
10. **Series C share count rounding.** 5,500,000 shares × $8.18 = $44,990,000, $10,000 short of $45,000,000 (computed). **Action:** set the definitive-document share count at $45,000,000 ÷ final price, not a rounded figure.

## 8. CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

1. The $8.18 price rests on a 22,004,000-share denominator that is **not supported by the Company's own records**; corrected denominators of 22,675,000–25,175,000 (depending on the pool-increase treatment and note-conversion reconciliation) imply prices of **$7.15–$7.94** (all computed). Because cap-table accuracy is a closing condition, these must be resolved before signing.
2. Reconcile the Series A ledger to the charter (3,500,000 vs. 3,820,616), the pool figure (1,275,000 vs. 1,204,000), and the warrant terms, and correct founder/board-designee names.
3. Resist or narrow the full ratchet, the 1.5x preference, and the §2.5(b)/(h)/(k) protective provisions as deviations from precedent.
4. Address the 409A ($8.75) vs. Series C ($8.18) valuation anomaly with the Board and the appraiser.
5. Ensure the Series C closing includes: charter amendment designating Series C out of the 9,000,000 undesignated Preferred; stockholder and Board approvals for the pool increase (Plan §6.1; Term Sheet §1.10); Ridgeline's joinder to the Voting Agreement and amended IRA; and drag-along/protective-provision consents of existing series.
