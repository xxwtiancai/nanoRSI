## 1. Executive Summary

Cascade Robotics, Inc. (the "Company"), a Delaware C-corporation, proposes to raise **$45,000,000** in Series C Preferred Stock at a **$180,000,000 pre-money / $225,000,000 post-money** valuation, led by Ridgeline Growth Partners Fund VI, L.P. ($35M), with $10M of pro rata participation by existing preferred holders Firstvale Ventures and Summit Arc Capital. The term sheet fixes the pre-money fully diluted share count at **22,004,000 shares**, yielding a price of **$8.18 per share** and approximately **5,500,000 new Series C shares**.

Our review of the Company's capitalization table, charter, financing documents, and the term sheet identifies **material discrepancies and internal inconsistencies** that affect the accuracy of the 22,004,000-share denominator, the $8.18 price, and closing conditions. Because the Company represents and warrants the accuracy of the cap table as a condition to closing (Term Sheet §§ 1.6, 1.11, 5.6), these items must be reconciled before definitive documents are signed. The Series C terms (1.5x senior non-participating preference, full ratchet anti-dilution, and expansive protective provisions) are also materially investor-favorable relative to the Series A/B precedent and warrant negotiation.

## 5. Discrepancies and Issues Requiring Resolution

### 5.1 Series A share count — 3,500,000 vs. 3,820,616
The Restated Charter (§§ 4.2(b), 5A.1) states that **3,820,616** Series A shares are issued and outstanding, including **320,616 shares issued on conversion of the Firstvale note** at a $2.40 conversion price ($769,479.45 of principal and interest). The cap table and term sheet use only **3,500,000** Series A shares and expressly note that "convertible note conversion shares not reflected." If the charter is correct, the fully diluted denominator is understated by 320,616 shares and the $8.18 price is overstated. Obtain the transfer agent's (Ashford Financial Services) stock ledger and reconcile.

### 5.2 Fully diluted denominator omissions (22,004,000)
- **450,000 exercised-option common shares** are omitted from the term sheet buildup (which uses 10,000,000 founders' common rather than 10,450,000 outstanding).
- **150,000 Pinnacle warrant shares** are omitted, even though Term Sheet § 1.5 states the pre-money calculation is inclusive of "all outstanding ... warrants."
- **Available pool figure conflict:** the Option Grants tab computes **1,275,000** available (5,000,000 − 3,725,000 granted), while the term sheet and FD buildup use **1,204,000** (an unexplained 71,000-share difference). Note also that because the 175,000 forfeited shares reverted to the pool under Plan § 2.3, available reserves may arguably be 1,450,000 (5,000,000 − 3,550,000 net outstanding grants).

A corrected denominator (including 450,000 exercised shares, 320,616 note-conversion shares, warrant shares, and the true pool figure) would exceed **23,000,000 shares**, reducing the price below $8.00 and increasing Series C shares issued. Because cap table accuracy is a closing condition (§§ 1.6, 1.11, 5.6), this must be resolved.

### 5.3 Option pool increase inconsistency (Term Sheet § 1.10)
Section 1.10 requires a pre-closing pool increase from 5,000,000 to 7,500,000 (+2,500,000) and states the increase "shall be included in the pre-money Fully Diluted Capitalization" — but the stated 22,004,000 denominator already incorporates only the existing 1,204,000-share pool. If the increase is genuinely included pre-money, the denominator should be ~24,500,000+ and the price ~$7.34; if not, the increase dilutes only existing holders post-pricing. The two provisions are internally inconsistent and must be conformed in the definitive documents.

### 5.4 Warrant terms conflict
Cap table/Warrants tab/409A report: Pinnacle National Bank warrant dated **June 22, 2021**, exercise price **$3.00**. Restated Charter § 4.2(e): warrant dated **March 10, 2022**, exercise price **$3.50**. Confirm the actual warrant instrument.

### 5.5 Name and board-seat inconsistencies across documents
- Founder/CTO: "Raj **Venkatesh**" (term sheet, cap table) vs. "Raj **Subramanian**" (convertible note recitals, Voting Agreement Exhibit B). Confirm legal name and align stock ledger records.
- Series A Director: **Allison Trang** (IRA, Voting Agreement, 409A) vs. **Thomas Aldridge** (cap table Series A tab).
- Series B Director: **Derek Hollowell** (IRA, Voting Agreement, 409A) vs. **Victoria Langford** (cap table Series B tab).

### 5.6 Series C protective provisions vs. 2019 EIP
Term Sheet § 2.5(b) requires Series C majority consent for **any** equity plan issuance, "including any ... option ... granted under any equity incentive plan, **whether or not previously approved by the Board**." This overrides the Plan's administrative structure (Plan §§ 3.2, 6.1: routine grants within the reserve require no separate stockholder/series approval) and effectively gives Ridgeline a veto over every option grant. The Company should negotiate to conform § 2.5(b) to the Excluded Issuances carve-out in § 2.3.3(i) (EIP issuances approved by a board including a Series C designee).

### 5.7 Pricing below 409A FMV
The $8.18 Series C preferred price is below the $8.75 409A common FMV (9/30/2024). While a preferred price below a stale-dated common 409A is not per se improper, the 409A report predates the term sheet (November 8, 2024) and the appraiser noted the pending Series C only as a subsequent event. A fresh 409A will likely be needed post-closing, affecting future option pricing.

### 5.8 Charter authorization capacity
Current charter: 30,000,000 common; 20,000,000 preferred (5,000,000 Series A; 6,000,000 Series B; 9,000,000 undesignated). Common reserves (10,450,000 issued + 5,000,000 pool + 150,000 warrant + conversion reserves of ~7,700,000–8,020,616) leave adequate but tightening capacity; the pool increase to 7,500,000 and the 5,500,000-share Series C issuance (within the 9,000,000 undesignated preferred) require the Restated Charter amendment contemplated by the term sheet. Board approval expected November 22, 2024; target closing December 31, 2024.

## 6. Key Series C Terms — Comparison to Precedent

| Term | Series A | Series B | Series C (Proposed) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price / OIP | $3.00 | $6.00 | $8.18 |
| Liquidation preference | 1x non-participating | 1x non-participating | **1.5x non-participating, senior to A/B** |
| Anti-dilution | Broad-based weighted average | Broad-based weighted average | **Full ratchet** |
| Board seat | 1 director | 1 director | Director designation contemplated (per § 2.3.3 Excluded Issuances references) |
| Automatic conversion | ≥$30M IPO at ≥$9.00 (3x) | Per Series B SPA | ≥$50M IPO at ≥$16.36 (2x) |

Notable investor-favorable deviations: (i) **full ratchet** anti-dilution (vs. weighted average in all prior rounds) is aggressive for an up-round priced 36% above Series B; (ii) the **1.5x senior preference** creates a $67.5M stack senior to $35.7M of existing preferences — total preferences of ~$103.2M against a $225M post-money; (iii) protective provisions extend to executive hiring/compensation, budget approval, and >$5M debt; and (iv) pro rata participation ($10M) is limited to existing preferred — Firstvale and Summit Arc allocations to be settled per the IRA pro rata provisions, with unsubscribed amounts reallocated at the Lead Investor's discretion.

## 7. Recommended Action Items

1. **Reconcile the Series A share count** (3,500,000 vs. 3,820,616) with the transfer agent ledger and Exhibit A to the Restated Charter; correct the cap table.
2. **Rebuild the fully diluted denominator** to include the 450,000 exercised shares, 150,000 warrant shares, and the correct available pool figure; recompute the per-share price and share count.
3. **Conform Term Sheet §§ 1.5/1.6/1.10** regarding whether the 2,500,000-share pool increase is inside or outside the pre-money calculation.
4. **Confirm the Pinnacle warrant** date/exercise price and obtain the underlying instrument.
5. **Resolve name discrepancies** (Venkatesh/Subramanian) and board designee records (Trang/Aldridge; Hollowell/Langford).
6. **Negotiate the protective provisions** (§ 2.5(b), (h), (k)) and consider pushing full ratchet to broad-based weighted average, consistent with Series A/B precedent.
7. **Plan for a post-closing 409A refresh** given the pricing anomaly and new capital structure.
8. **Confirm board/stockholder approvals** for the pool increase, Restated Charter amendment, and share authorization ahead of the November 22, 2024 board meeting.
