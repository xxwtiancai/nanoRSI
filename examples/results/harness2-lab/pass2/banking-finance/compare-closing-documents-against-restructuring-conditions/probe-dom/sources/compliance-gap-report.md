# Compliance Gap Report — Ridgewater Holdings Restructuring

**Prepared for:** Ad Hoc Group of Term Loan Lenders (Stonebridge Capital Management, Clearfield Asset Partners, LP, Northvane Credit Opportunities Fund)
**Prepared by:** Ashworth, Beckett & Lowe LLP, Ad Hoc Group Counsel
**Subject:** Review of closing documents against the Restructuring Support Agreement dated April 28, 2025 (the "RSA") and the Restructuring Term Sheet attached as Exhibit A (the "Term Sheet")
**Closing Date Reviewed:** July 14, 2025

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The closing document set contains numerous material discrepancies when measured against the RSA and Term Sheet, including several that go to the core economics of the exchange (facility size, pricing, maturities, equity splits, MIP dilution, and board control) and at least one condition precedent (minimum participation) that appears **not to have been satisfied** as certified. Items are classified below as **Critical** (economic/structural deviations or failed conditions precedent), **High** (material inconsistencies requiring correction or waiver), or **Note** (drafting/minor items).

---

## 2. Critical Findings

### 2.1 Minimum Participation Condition Not Satisfied (Failed Condition Precedent)
- **RSA Requirement:** Minimum Participation Threshold of **95%** of the outstanding Existing TLB (not less than $449,350,000) (RSA §§1, 7(f)).
- **Closing Documents:** The Exchange Agreement (§3.01) certifies the Participating Amount at **$446,200,000, or 94.34%** — approximately $3.15 million short of the threshold. $26,800,000 (5.66%) remains with Non-Exchanging Lenders.
- **Compounding Issue:** The Officer's Certificate (§4(f)) certifies that "holders of not less than 95%… have validly tendered." This certification is **contradicted by the Exchange Agreement's own certified results** and appears to be a false certification.
- **Action Required:** Closing should not proceed absent (i) cure of the shortfall (e.g., subsequent exchange offer / open-market purchases as contemplated in Exchange Agreement §3.03), or (ii) an express written waiver of the 95% condition by the Required Consenting Lenders under the RSA waiver provisions.

### 2.2 New TL Pricing Deviates from RSA
- **RSA Requirement:** New First Lien Term Loans bear interest at **SOFR + 550 bps** (RSA §§1, 2.1(a); Exchange Agreement §4.02(a) is consistent).
- **Closing Document:** The New TL Credit Agreement defines "Applicable Margin" as **5.00% per annum (500 bps)** — a 50 bps reduction in lender economics.
- **Action Required:** Correct the Applicable Margin to 5.50% or obtain Required Consenting Lender consent to the deviation.

### 2.3 New 2L Notes Maturity Conflict / Loss of 1L-2L Maturity Separation
- **RSA Requirement:** New 2L Notes mature **October 28, 2030** — six months after the New TL maturity of April 28, 2030.
- **Closing Documents:** The New TL Credit Agreement defines the "New 2L Notes Maturity Date" as **April 28, 2030** — the same day as the first lien maturity, eliminating the intended maturity separation. The Exchange Agreement (§4.02(b)) is consistent with the RSA (October 28, 2030), creating an inter-document conflict.
- **Action Required:** Correct the New TL Credit Agreement definition to October 28, 2030.

### 2.4 New 2L Notes Principal Amount Inconsistencies
- **RSA Requirement:** New 2L Notes aggregate principal of **$95,000,000**.
- **Closing Documents:**
  - New RCF Credit Agreement defines the New 2L Notes as having an aggregate original principal amount of **$210,000,000**.
  - Officer's Certificate (§3, item 2) describes the New 2L Notes as **$130,000,000**.
  - Company Counsel Opinion describes an 11.50%/12.25% second lien note "due 2031" (see §2.8 below).
- **Action Required:** Conform all documents to $95,000,000 / 10.00% cash–12.00% PIK / due October 28, 2030.

### 2.5 New TL Facility Size Misstated in Officer's Certificate
- **RSA Requirement / New TL Credit Agreement / Exchange Agreement:** New TL of **$280,000,000**.
- **Officer's Certificate (§3, item 1):** describes the New TL facility as **$285,000,000**.
- **Action Required:** Correct the Officer's Certificate; re-execute if relied upon at closing.

### 2.6 MIP Deviates from RSA in Three Material Respects
RSA §1 ("MIP") requires: (a) reserve of **not more than 8%** of fully diluted Pro Forma Equity; (b) **50/50** time-based/performance-based allocation; (c) vesting over **four (4) years with a one (1)-year cliff**; performance metrics set by the New Board subject to Required Consenting Lender approval.

The adopted MIP instead provides:
- **Aggregate Share Reserve of 9.5%** (MIP §§2.1, 4.1) — 150 bps above the RSA cap; direct additional dilution to the Ad Hoc Group's 30% equity stake.
- **60% time-based / 40% performance-based** split (MIP §§5.1, 5.2) — deviates from the 50/50 requirement.
- **Time-based awards vest over three (3) years with a six (6)-month cliff** (MIP §5.1) — deviates from the 4-year / 1-year cliff requirement.
- Additional drafting note: the MIP Effective Date is blank ("[●], 2025"), and Common Stock par value is stated as $0.01 while the Equity Shareholders Agreement states $0.001.
- **Action Required:** Amend the MIP to conform to the RSA, or obtain express Required Consenting Lender approval of the deviations as the RSA's "form and substance acceptable" standard requires.

### 2.7 Board Composition Deviates from RSA
- **RSA Requirement:** New Board of seven: **three (3) Ad Hoc Group designees** (one each from Stonebridge, Clearfield, Northvane), two (2) Backstop designees, CEO, and one independent.
- **Equity Shareholders Agreement (§3.01(a)):** provides only **two (2) Ad Hoc Group designees** and **three (3) Backstop Party designees** — a transfer of a board seat away from the Ad Hoc Group.
- **Compounding Issue:** The Officer's Certificate (§4(i)) certifies that the Board was reconstituted "in accordance with Section 7(i) of the RSA," which is inconsistent with the Equity Shareholders Agreement as drafted.
- **Action Required:** Amend the Equity Shareholders Agreement board provisions to restore three Ad Hoc Group seats.

### 2.8 Company Counsel Opinion Describes Different 2L Notes
- **Company Counsel Opinion (§2, item 3):** describes the New 2L Indenture as providing **"11.50%/12.25%… Notes due 2031"** — inconsistent with the RSA (10.00%/12.00%, due October 28, 2030) and every other closing document.
- The Opinion also describes the ES APA as "dated as of the date hereof" (July 14, 2025), whereas the executed ES APA is dated July 8, 2025.
- **Action Required:** Obtain a corrected/conformed opinion. Because the opinion is a Section 7(e) condition precedent deliverable that must be "in form and substance reasonably acceptable to the Required Consenting Lenders," the current draft is unacceptable.

### 2.9 Environmental Solutions Sale — Net Cash Proceeds Condition Appears Unmet
- **RSA Requirement:** Environmental Solutions Sale Agreement must provide **Net Cash Proceeds of not less than $55,000,000**. The RSA definition of Net Cash Proceeds expressly **excludes escrowed/held-back amounts until released** and **deducts transaction costs and taxes**.
- **ES APA Terms:** Purchase Price of $58,000,000, with a $4,200,000 escrow (18-month release date), estimated transaction costs of $1,100,000, plus taxes and possible working capital true-downs. Net Cash Proceeds at closing are approximately **$52.7 million or less** (58.0 − 4.2 − 1.1, before taxes) — below the $55 million floor.
- **Compounding Issue:** The Officer's Certificate (§4(g)) certifies that "the purchase price under the ES APA is $58,000,000, which satisfies the minimum net cash proceeds requirement of $55,000,000" — conflating gross price with the RSA's Net Cash Proceeds standard.
- **Additional Note:** The ES APA has not closed (Outside Date October 10, 2025); the RSA condition is execution of a binding APA meeting the Net Cash Proceeds floor, which is not satisfied on the face of the documents.
- **Action Required:** Seek price/escrow/cost adjustments, or waive the condition with eyes open as to the economics.

### 2.10 Minimum Liquidity Certification Is Internally Inconsistent
- **RSA Requirement:** Minimum Liquidity of not less than **$60,000,000** (unrestricted cash of the Company **and its Subsidiaries** plus available New RCF commitments).
- **Officer's Certificate (§4(h)):** certifies $22,000,000 cash + **$45,000,000** available RCF = $67,000,000.
- **New RCF Credit Agreement (§2.01(b)):** the Closing Date Revolving Loan is **$47,000,000**, leaving available commitments of **$38,000,000** — not $45,000,000. On the RCF's own numbers, liquidity is $60,000,000 — exactly at the minimum with no cushion, and the $67 million certification is unsupported.
- **Additional Deviation:** The RSA contemplates an initial Closing Date draw of **$40,000,000**; the RCF documents provide for **$47,000,000**. The New RCF's "Minimum Liquidity" definition also counts only **domestic** Subsidiaries' cash, narrower than the RSA.
- **Action Required:** Reconcile draw amounts and re-certify; conform the liquidity definition.

## 3. High-Priority Findings

### 3.1 Subsidiary Guarantor Jurisdiction Errors in Exchange Agreement
The Exchange Agreement (§1.01) misstates jurisdictions of incorporation for three guarantors relative to the RSA and all other closing documents:
| Guarantor | RSA / Other Documents | Exchange Agreement |
|---|---|---|
| Ridgewater Industrial Services, Inc. | North Carolina | "a Delaware corporation" |
| Tidemark Coatings, Inc. | Ohio | "a North Carolina corporation" |
| Ridgewater Logistics, Inc. | Georgia | "a Delaware corporation" |

These errors implicate guarantee enforceability and lien perfection for the affected entities and must be corrected.

### 3.2 Backstop Commitment Agreement Date Conflict
- New TL Credit Agreement: Backstop Commitment Agreement dated **May 12, 2025**.
- Equity Shareholders Agreement: Backstop Commitment Agreement dated **May 30, 2025**.
Confirm which instrument (if either) is in effect and conform references.

### 3.3 Equity Split Dilution Math / Noteholder Warrants
- The Equity Shareholders Agreement allocates 55% Backstop / 30% Lender Shareholders / 15% Noteholder Shareholders **plus warrants for an additional 5%** struck at 1.5x plan equity value. The RSA (as excerpted) provides 55% to the Backstop Commitment Parties and 30% to exchanging lenders, with the noteholder treatment and warrant terms set in the Term Sheet. Confirm the 15% + 5% warrant package conforms to the Term Sheet and that the fully diluted math (including the over-sized 9.5% MIP — see §2.6) is consistent across documents.

## 4. Notes / Minor Items

1. **Officer's Certificate deliverables description (§3, item 4)** describes the Exchange Agreement as among "the Company, Reorganized Ridgewater Holdings, Inc., and each of the exchanging lenders"; the executed Exchange Agreement is among Ridgewater Holdings, Inc., the Subsidiary Guarantors, the Agent, and the Exchanging Lenders.
2. **Officer's Certificate numbering** skips subsection (k) in Section 4 (runs (j) to (l)); confirm no certification was omitted.
3. **New RCF pricing** (Adjusted Term SOFR + 400 bps + 0.10% credit spread adjustment; 50 bps commitment fee) could not be tied to the excerpted Term Sheet provisions; confirm conformance.
4. **MIP par value ($0.01)** vs. Equity Shareholders Agreement par value ($0.001) for Common Stock.
5. **MIP Effective Date** left blank ("[●], 2025"); must be completed as the Closing Date (July 14, 2025).
6. **Compliant items confirmed:** RSA execution (April 28, 2025); Exchange Offer launch (May 16) and expiration (June 16) within milestones; Closing Date (July 14) prior to Outside Date (July 15); intercompany claims of $11.8M below the $12.0M cap with subordination agreements delivered; Section 3(a)(9) exemption consistently asserted; Ridgewater Europe GmbH consistently excluded as a non-guarantor; ES APA buyer (Greenfield) as specified in the RSA.

## 6. Recommended Next Steps

1. **Do not waive or fund** until the Critical items (§2) are resolved or expressly waived in writing by the Required Consenting Lenders in accordance with the RSA.
2. Demand conformed/amended versions of: New TL Credit Agreement (margin; 2L maturity definition), New RCF Credit Agreement (2L principal; draw amount), Exchange Agreement (guarantor jurisdictions), Equity Shareholders Agreement (board composition), MIP (reserve %, split, vesting, par value, effective date), Officer's Certificate (facility sizes, participation, ES proceeds, liquidity), and Company Counsel Opinion (2L terms; ES APA date).
3. Require written confirmation of the true Exchange Offer participation and, if below 95%, a documented waiver or cure mechanism before reliance on the Officer's Certificate.
4. Obtain a revised Net Cash Proceeds calculation for the ES Sale demonstrating compliance with the RSA definition (net of escrow, costs, and taxes), or negotiate remedy.
5. Confirm the operative Backstop Commitment Agreement date and the Term Sheet conformity of the noteholder 15% + 5% warrant package.

*This report is based solely on the excerpted documents provided and is subject to the full executed documents, the complete Term Sheet, and any RSA amendments or waivers not included in the review set.*
