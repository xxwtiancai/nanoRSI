# Compliance Gap Report
## Closing Document Review — Ridgewater Holdings, Inc. Restructuring
**Prepared for:** Ad Hoc Group of Term Loan B Lenders (Stonebridge Capital Management, Clearfield Asset Partners, LP, Northvane Credit Opportunities Fund)
**Prepared by:** Ashworth, Beckett & Lowe LLP, Ad Hoc Group Counsel
**Date:** July 2025
**Benchmark:** Restructuring Support Agreement dated April 28, 2025 (the "RSA") and Restructuring Term Sheet (Exhibit A thereto)

---

## I. Executive Summary

A review of the closing deliverables against the RSA and Term Sheet reveals **material discrepancies** in nearly every closing document, including (i) an apparent **failure of the Minimum Participation Threshold** contradicted by an Officer's Certificate that certifies satisfaction, (ii) wrong principal amounts, interest rates, and maturities for the new debt instruments across multiple documents, (iii) an **oversized Management Incentive Plan** (9.5% vs. the 8% RSA cap) with non-conforming vesting terms, (iv) a **reconfigured Board** that reduces Ad Hoc Group designation rights, (v) an ES APA whose Net Cash Proceeds appear to fall **below the $55 million RSA minimum**, and (vi) inconsistent factual descriptions in the Company Counsel opinion and Officer's Certificate. Items 1–5 below are, in our view, closing-blocker-grade issues requiring correction or Required Consenting Lender waiver before funding.

---

## II. Critical Discrepancies

### 1. Minimum Participation Threshold — Exchange Agreement vs. Officer's Certificate (CRITICAL)
- **RSA requirement:** Minimum Participation Threshold of **95%** of outstanding Existing TLB (i.e., not less than **$449,350,000**) (RSA Section 1 definitions; Exchange Agreement "Minimum Participation Condition").
- **Exchange Agreement, Section 3.01:** Participating Amount certified at **$446,200,000 = 94.34%**, with $26,800,000 (5.66%) held by Non-Exchanging Lenders. **The threshold was missed by $3.15 million.**
- **Officer's Certificate, Section 4(f):** Certifies that "not less than 95%" tendered and that the Exchange Agent confirmed the threshold was met. **This certification is contradicted by the Exchange Agreement's own certified results and appears to be inaccurate.** Closing over an unsatisfied RSA condition precedent requires an express waiver by the Required Consenting Lenders.

### 2. New First Lien Term Loan — Pricing and Amount Errors
- **Interest rate:** RSA/Term Sheet and Exchange Agreement: **SOFR + 550 bps**. New TL Credit Agreement "Applicable Margin": **5.00% (500 bps)** — a 50 bps shortfall to lenders.
- **Principal amount:** Officer's Certificate Section 3(1) describes the New TL facility as **$285,000,000**, not the $280,000,000 set forth in the RSA, the New TL Credit Agreement itself, and the Exchange Agreement.
- Also note: the New TL Credit Agreement uses daily SOFR while the Exchange Agreement/New TL delivery description references **Term SOFR** + 550 bps — conform.

### 3. New Second Lien PIK Toggle Notes — Four Inconsistent Descriptions
| Source | Principal | Rate | Maturity |
|---|---|---|---|
| RSA / Term Sheet | $95,000,000 | 10.00% cash / 12.00% PIK | October 28, 2030 |
| New TL Credit Agreement | $95,000,000 | — | **April 28, 2030** (wrong; eliminates the 6-month gap behind the New TL) |
| New RCF Credit Agreement | **$210,000,000** (wrong) | 10%/12% | October 28, 2030 |
| Officer's Certificate §3(2) | **$130,000,000** (wrong) | — | — |
| Company Counsel Opinion §2(3) | $95,000,000 | **11.50% / 12.25%** (wrong) | **2031** (wrong) |

### 4. Management Incentive Plan — Multiple RSA Violations
- **Share reserve:** MIP Sections 2.1 and 4.1: Aggregate Share Reserve of **9.5%** of fully diluted equity. RSA definition of "MIP" caps the reserve at **8%**. (The MIP's own Executive Summary recites "up to 8%" — internally inconsistent with Sections 2.1/4.1.)
- **Vesting allocation:** MIP: **60% time-based / 40% performance-based**. RSA: **50%/50%**.
- **Vesting schedule:** MIP time-based awards vest over **3 years with a 6-month cliff**. RSA: **4-year vesting with 1-year cliff**.
- **Form/acceptability:** The RSA requires the MIP to be "in form and substance acceptable to the Required Consenting Lenders" (RSA Section 7(j)); the Officer's Certificate so certifies, but the terms above have not been approved as so altered.
- **Housekeeping:** The MIP Effective Date is left blank ("[●]"), and it recites Common Stock par value of $0.01 while the Equity Shareholders Agreement recites $0.001.

### 5. Board Composition — Equity Shareholders Agreement vs. RSA
- **RSA "New Board" definition (7 members):** (a) **three (3)** directors designated by the Ad Hoc Group (one each by Stonebridge, Clearfield, and Northvane); (b) **two (2)** by Backstop Commitment Parties; (c) CEO; (d) one independent director **mutually agreed by the Company and the Required Consenting Lenders**.
- **Equity Shareholders Agreement Section 3.01(a):** only **two (2)** Ad Hoc Group designees (subject to forfeiture below 15% holdings); **three (3)** Backstop designees; independent director selected by the **Ad Hoc Group and Backstop Parties subject to CEO approval** — no Required Consenting Lender role.
- Additional non-RSA terms: designation-right reduction thresholds (15%/5%/25%), committee composition mandates, quorum requirements keyed to Ad Hoc/Backstop designees, and Backstop selection of the Chairman. These materially dilute the Ad Hoc Group's agreed governance rights and should be conformed to the RSA or expressly consented to.

### 6. Environmental Solutions Sale — Net Cash Proceeds Shortfall
- **RSA requirement:** Environmental Solutions Sale Agreement must provide **Net Cash Proceeds of not less than $55,000,000**, where "Net Cash Proceeds" expressly **excludes escrow/holdbacks until released** and is net of transaction costs and taxes.
- **ES APA:** Purchase Price $58,000,000, less Escrow Amount **$4,200,000** and Estimated Transaction Costs **$1,100,000** (before any taxes and before any working capital adjustment, which can go against Seller), yielding Net Cash Proceeds of at most approximately **$52.7 million** — **below the $55 million floor**.
- **Officer's Certificate Section 4(g)** erroneously certifies compliance by comparing the **gross** $58,000,000 purchase price against the **net** $55,000,000 requirement. This is a failed RSA condition precedent as documented (absent waiver).
- Other observations: ES APA Outside Date is October 10, 2025 (post-restructuring closing); Buyer assumes certain **environmental remediation liabilities** (Raleigh, NC) and up to $2.3 million of trade payables — the RSA's sale construct contemplated transfer of segment assets with proceeds to the Company; assumption of liabilities further erodes value and should be vetted against the Term Sheet economics. The New RCF's automatic **25% commitment reduction** upon the ES sale was not an agreed RSA term in the provisions reviewed and reduces Availability/Minimum Liquidity headroom.

### 7. New RCF — Closing Draw, Availability and Liquidity Inconsistencies
- **Initial draw:** RSA "New RCF" definition: initial Closing Date draw of **$40,000,000**. New RCF Credit Agreement Section 2.01(b): Closing Date Revolving Loan of **$47,000,000** (used in part to refinance the $67 million Existing RCF together with other sources).
- **Availability:** Per the New RCF Credit Agreement, post-draw availability is **$38,000,000** ($85M − $47M). The Officer's Certificate Section 4(h) certifies **$45,000,000** of undrawn availability and resulting Minimum Liquidity of $67,000,000 ($22M cash + $45M). On the documents' own figures, actual Minimum Liquidity would be **$60,000,000 ($22M + $38M)** — exactly at, with no cushion over, the $60 million RSA floor. The certificate's figures are inconsistent with the RCF Credit Agreement.
- **Definition variance:** New RCF "Minimum Liquidity" counts only **domestic** Subsidiaries' cash; the RSA definition covers cash of the Company **and its Subsidiaries** (with the control-agreement carve-out).
- **Pricing note:** The New RCF bears Adjusted Term SOFR (with a 0.10% credit spread adjustment) + 400 bps; confirm conformity to the Term Sheet's RCF economics (not fully visible in the excerpted RSA).

### 8. Exchange Agreement — Subsidiary Guarantor Descriptions Incorrect
The Exchange Agreement's "Subsidiary Guarantors" definition misstates jurisdictions of formation for three guarantors versus the RSA (and all other documents):
- Ridgewater Industrial Services, Inc.: listed as a **Delaware** corporation (correct: **North Carolina**);
- Tidemark Coatings, Inc.: listed as a **North Carolina** corporation (correct: **Ohio**);
- Ridgewater Logistics, Inc.: listed as a **Delaware** corporation (correct: **Georgia**).

### 9. Company Counsel Opinion — Errors and Inconsistencies
- Describes the New 2L Notes as **11.50%/12.25% Second Lien PIK Toggle Notes due 2031** — inconsistent with the RSA (10%/12%, due October 28, 2030), the Exchange Agreement, and the New RCF Credit Agreement.
- States the New TL facility is $280,000,000 (correct), but this conflicts with the Officer's Certificate ($285 million) delivered the same day — one of the two is wrong.
- Describes the ES APA as "dated as of the date hereof" (July 14, 2025); the executed ES APA is dated **July 8, 2025**.
- Reliance/benefit is appropriately addressed to the Ad Hoc Group and counsel; no opinion on German law (Ridgewater Europe GmbH) — consistent with RSA, but note the opinion relies on the Officer's Certificate (Section 3(e)), whose inaccuracies are itemized above.

### 10. Officer's Certificate — Additional Certification Gaps
- **Section 4(f)** mis-certifies minimum participation (see Item 1).
- **Section 4(g)** mis-applies the Net Cash Proceeds test (see Item 6).
- **Section 4(h)** liquidity figures inconsistent with the New RCF Credit Agreement (see Item 7).
- Section 3(1) and 3(2) misstate the New TL and New 2L principal amounts ($285M / $130M).
- The certificate omits a certification corresponding to RSA Section 7(k) (numbering skips from (j) to (l)); confirm whether a Section 7(k) condition exists and, if so, require its certification.
- Issued by the CFO — permitted under the RSA definition ("CFO or CEO"); the Exchange Agreement's reference to Section 7(d) should be conformed to the certificate's actual statutory reference (Section 7(d) per certificate; RSA Sections 7(a)–(n) structure differs from the certificate's own internal cross-references — conform citation).

### 11. Intercreditor and Other Deliverables
- The **Amended & Restated Intercreditor Agreement** is a defined Restructuring Document under the RSA and a listed Loan Document in the New TL Credit Agreement ("if and when executed"), but its execution is not certified anywhere in the Officer's Certificate deliverables checklist. **Confirm execution and delivery** of an intercreditor agreement in form acceptable to the Agent, the Required Consenting Lenders, and Thorncastle as New RCF lender; the New RCF Credit Agreement purports to establish the three-tier lien waterfall unilaterally (super-priority RCF / 1L TL / 2L Notes) — the agreed intercreditor mechanics (standstill, enforcement, adequate protection) must not be varied.
- **Backstop Commitment Agreement date conflict:** New TL Credit Agreement recites it as dated **May 12, 2025**; Equity Shareholders Agreement recites **May 30, 2025**. Reconcile.
- **Equity split confirmation:** The Equity Shareholders Agreement allocates 55% Backstop / 30% Lender Shareholders / 15% Noteholder Shareholders (plus warrants for an additional 5%). The visible RSA provisions confirm the 55% Backstop allocation; confirm the 30%/15% split and warrant terms against Term Sheet Section 2 (truncated in the version provided).
- **New TL Adjusted EBITDA:** includes a $35 million cap on restructuring-cost addbacks and loss/gain on disposition adjustments — not an RSA-agreed term; economic impact on leverage covenants should be confirmed acceptable.
- **Existing Notes treatment:** RSA contemplates cancellation of the Existing Notes; the Shareholders Agreement shows noteholders receiving 15% equity plus warrants. Confirm the Exchange/consent solicitation mechanics for the Notes conform to the Term Sheet.
- **Minimum Liquidity carve-out:** RSA excludes cash subject to control agreements in favor of non-Agent persons; verify the New RCF/closing certificates apply the same carve-out.

## IV. Recommended Actions

1. **Do not permit closing** until the Minimum Participation failure (Item 1) is resolved — either by securing additional tenders/open-market purchases to reach $449,350,000, or by an express written waiver of the condition by the Required Consenting Lenders (and amendment of the Exchange Agreement's pro rata mechanics to address the $26.8 million stub).
2. **Correct the New TL Credit Agreement Applicable Margin to 550 bps** and conform the Officer's Certificate to the $280 million principal amount.
3. **Conform all documents to the $95 million / 10%–12% / October 28, 2030 New 2L Notes terms** (New TL CA, New RCF CA, Officer's Certificate, Company Counsel opinion); require a corrected opinion bring-down.
4. **Amend the MIP** to an 8% reserve, 50/50 allocation, 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff, or obtain express Required Consenting Lender approval of the deviations; complete the Effective Date.
5. **Amend the Equity Shareholders Agreement board provisions** to restore three Ad Hoc Group designees, two Backstop designees, and Required Consenting Lender participation in the independent director selection.
6. **Re-test the ES APA against the $55 million Net Cash Proceeds floor** on a true net basis; if short, require price adjustment, escrow/cost restructuring, or Required Consenting Lender waiver; correct Officer's Certificate Section 4(g).
7. **Obtain a corrected Officer's Certificate** (participation, liquidity, facility amounts) and a **corrected Company Counsel opinion** (2L terms, ES APA date).
8. **Confirm execution of the Amended & Restated Intercreditor Agreement** acceptable to the Required Consenting Lenders, and reconcile the Backstop Commitment Agreement date.
9. **Fix the Exchange Agreement guarantor jurisdictions** and conform benchmark (Term vs. daily SOFR) references.
10. **Confirm Minimum Liquidity at closing** on the correct availability figure ($38M) and confirm no conditions trip the $60 million floor post-closing (including the RCF's 25% ES-sale commitment reduction).

*This report is based solely on the documents provided for review; certain RSA/Term Sheet provisions were truncated in the versions provided and are flagged above for confirmation against executed originals.*
