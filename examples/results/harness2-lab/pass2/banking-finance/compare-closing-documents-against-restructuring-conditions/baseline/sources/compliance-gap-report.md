# COMPLIANCE GAP REPORT
## Ridgewater Holdings, Inc. — Restructuring Closing Document Review

**Prepared for:** Ad Hoc Group of Term Loan B Lenders (Stonebridge Capital Management; Clearfield Asset Partners, LP; Northvane Credit Opportunities Fund)
**Prepared by:** Ashworth, Beckett & Lowe LLP, as Ad Hoc Group Counsel
**Benchmark Documents:** Restructuring Support Agreement dated April 28, 2025 (the "RSA") and Restructuring Term Sheet (Exhibit A)
**Closing Documents Reviewed:** New TL Credit Agreement; New RCF Credit Agreement; Exchange Agreement; ES Asset Purchase Agreement; Equity Shareholders Agreement; Management Incentive Plan; Officer's Certificate; Company Counsel Legal Opinion
**Date of Review:** July 14, 2025 (targeted Closing Date)

---

## I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The closing set contains **material discrepancies** against the RSA and Term Sheet. Two issues appear to be closing blockers:

1. **The Minimum Participation Threshold (95%) has NOT been met** — actual participation was 94.34% ($446.2M of $473M) — yet the Officer's Certificate certifies satisfaction of the condition.
2. **The ES APA does not satisfy the $55M Net Cash Proceeds condition** — gross price is $58M, but escrow ($4.2M) and transaction costs (~$1.1M, plus taxes) reduce Net Cash Proceeds to well below $55M under the RSA's Net Cash Proceeds definition.

In addition, the Officer's Certificate misstates facility amounts, the New TL and New RCF Credit Agreements contain pricing/maturity/principal errors, the Equity Shareholders Agreement re-allocates board seats away from the Ad Hoc Group, and the MIP exceeds the RSA's 8% share reserve cap and uses non-conforming vesting terms. Do not permit funding/closing until the items marked "Critical" below are resolved or expressly waived by the Required Consenting Lenders.

## II. CRITICAL DISCREPANCIES

### 1. Minimum Participation Threshold — NOT SATISFIED (RSA §§7(f), 5(d); "Minimum Participation Threshold" definition)
- **RSA Requirement:** Valid tender of ≥95% of Existing TLB, i.e., ≥$449,350,000.
- **Exchange Agreement §3.01:** Participating amount = **$446,200,000 = 94.34%** (Non-Exchanging Lenders hold $26.8M). Shortfall: **$3,150,000**.
- **Officer's Certificate §4(f):** Certifies the 95% threshold "has been satisfied in all respects" — **this certification is factually incorrect on the face of the closing set.**
- **Impact:** Condition precedent failure; potential RSA Termination Event; the false certification also undermines the reliance assumptions in the Company Counsel opinion (§3(e)).
- **Action Required:** Either (a) obtain additional tenders to reach 95%, (b) obtain written waiver from the Required Consenting Lenders under the RSA, or (c) exercise termination rights.

### 2. Environmental Solutions Sale — Net Cash Proceeds Below $55M Floor (RSA §7(g); "Net Cash Proceeds" definition)
- **RSA Requirement:** ES Sale Agreement must provide Net Cash Proceeds of not less than **$55,000,000**, where escrow/holdbacks, transaction costs, and taxes are deducted (escrowed amounts count only upon release).
- **ES APA:** Purchase Price $58,000,000; Escrow $4,200,000 (18 months); Estimated Transaction Costs $1,100,000; plus taxes and working capital adjustment exposure (Target $6.8M).
- **Calculation:** $58.0M − $4.2M escrow − $1.1M costs = **~$52.7M before taxes** — below the $55M floor even before tax deductions and any downward working capital adjustment.
- **Officer's Certificate §4(g):** Asserts the $58M "purchase price" satisfies the $55M minimum — this conflates **gross price** with **Net Cash Proceeds** and is inconsistent with the RSA definition.
- **Action Required:** Reduce escrow, increase price, or obtain Required Consenting Lender waiver.

### 3. Officer's Certificate Misstates New Facility Amounts (§3, Items 1–2)
- Certifies the New TL Credit Agreement at **$285,000,000** (actual/RSA: **$280,000,000**) and the New 2L Notes at **$130,000,000** (actual/RSA: **$95,000,000**).
- **Action Required:** Re-execute corrected certificate. These errors also infect the Company Counsel opinion's reliance on the certificate (opinion §3(e)).

### 4. New TL Credit Agreement — Applicable Margin Wrong
- **RSA / Term Sheet / Exchange Agreement:** SOFR + **550 bps**.
- **New TL CA §1.01:** "Applicable Margin" = **5.00% (500 bps)**.
- **Action Required:** Amend to 550 bps pre-closing; direct economic term deviation.

### 5. New TL Credit Agreement — 2L Notes Maturity Misstated
- **RSA / Term Sheet:** New 2L Notes mature **October 28, 2030** (six months after New TL maturity).
- **New TL CA §1.01 ("New 2L Notes Maturity Date"):** **April 28, 2030** — eliminating the 6-month tranche separation.
- **Action Required:** Correct to October 28, 2030.

### 6. New RCF Credit Agreement — 2L Notes Principal Misstated
- **RSA / Term Sheet:** New 2L Notes = **$95,000,000**.
- **New RCF CA §1.01 ("New Second Lien PIK Toggle Notes"):** **$210,000,000** aggregate original principal.
- **Action Required:** Correct definition; as drafted it misdescribes the debt stack and priority/waterfall references.

### 7. Equity Shareholders Agreement — Board Composition Flips RSA Allocation
- **RSA ("New Board" definition): 7 members = 3 Ad Hoc Group designees; 2 Backstop Commitment Party designees; 1 CEO (initially Martin Ashford); 1 independent director mutually agreed by the Company and Required Consenting Lenders.**
- **SHA §3.01(a): 2 Ad Hoc Group designees; 3 Backstop Party designees; 1 CEO; 1 independent.**
- Additional deviations: (i) independent director selection changed to Ad Hoc Group/Backstop agreement "subject to the reasonable approval of the CEO" (RSA: mutually agreed by the Company and Required Consenting Lenders); (ii) Chairman selected solely by the Backstop Parties; (iii) designation-right thresholds (15% AHG / 25% Backstop) and conversion of forfeited seats to additional independent seats have no RSA basis.
- **Impact:** Direct dilution of Ad Hoc Group governance rights bargained for in the RSA.

### 8. Management Incentive Plan — Exceeds 8% Cap; Non-Conforming Vesting
- **RSA ("MIP" definition):** Reserve of not more than **8%** of fully diluted Pro Forma Equity; **4-year vesting with 1-year cliff**; **50% time-based / 50% performance-based**; performance metrics subject to Required Consenting Lender approval.
- **MIP as adopted:**
  - §§2.1, 4.1: Aggregate Share Reserve = **9.5%** (the MIP's own Executive Summary recites 8% — internally inconsistent as well as RSA-non-compliant);
  - §5.1: Time-based awards = **60%**, vesting over **3 years with a 6-month cliff**;
  - §5.2: Performance-based awards = **40%**.
- **Also:** The Effective Date is blank ("[●], 2025") — the plan is undated, and the RSA requires adoption **prior to** the Closing Date (the Officer's Certificate recites adoption "on" the Closing Date).
- **Also:** §5.2 performance metrics are to be set by the Compensation Committee within 90 days post-closing, with no express Required Consenting Lender approval right at metric-setting (consent right appears only for future reserve increases, §3.3).
- **Action Required:** Reduce reserve to ≤8%, restore 4-year/1-year-cliff and 50/50 allocation, and obtain RCL sign-off.

## III. MATERIAL DISCREPANCIES

### 9. New RCF — Closing Date Draw Exceeds RSA Amount; Liquidity Certification Inconsistent
- **RSA ("New RCF" definition):** Initial Closing Date draw = **$40,000,000**.
- **New RCF CA §2.01(b):** Closing Date Revolving Loan = **$47,000,000**; resulting availability = **$38,000,000**.
- **Officer's Certificate §4(h):** Certifies cash of $22M plus RCF availability of **$45M** = $67M Minimum Liquidity, "exceeding the minimum by $7,000,000." Under the RCF as drafted, availability is $38M, yielding Minimum Liquidity of **$60M — exactly at the floor with zero cushion**, and the "exceeds by $7,000,000" statement is arithmetically wrong.
- **Also:** the RCF's "Minimum Liquidity" definition covers only "domestic Subsidiaries," and the RSA's exclusion of cash subject to control agreements in favor of non-Agent persons is not carried through.
- **Action Required:** Reconcile draw amount; re-run liquidity certification; conform definitions.

### 10. Company Counsel Opinion — 2L Notes Coupon/Maturity Misdescribed; ES APA Date Wrong
- **Opinion §2(3):** Describes the New 2L Notes as **"11.50%/12.25%"** Senior Secured Second Lien PIK Toggle Notes **due 2031** — inconsistent with the RSA/Exchange Agreement terms (**10.00% cash / 12.00% PIK, due October 28, 2030**).
- **Opinion §2(8):** Describes the ES APA as "dated as of the date hereof" (July 14, 2025); the executed ES APA is dated **July 8, 2025**.
- **Opinion §3(h):** Assumes an HSR exemption based on qualification for "one or more exemptions," vs. the Officer's Certificate's categorical "no filing required" statement — reconcile the stated basis.
- **Action Required:** Re-issue a corrected opinion; the enforceability opinion does not accurately cover the documents as executed.

### 11. New TL CA — ES APA Date Misstated
- **New TL CA ("Environmental Solutions Disposition"):** References an APA "dated as of **June 2, 2025**"; the executed ES APA is dated **July 8, 2025**. (A June 2 signing would also have pre-dated Exchange Offer expiration — likely a drafting error, but must be corrected.)

### 12. Exchange Agreement — Subsidiary Guarantor Jurisdictional Errors (§1.01)
- Ridgewater Industrial Services, Inc. listed as "a **Delaware** corporation" (RSA: North Carolina);
- Tidemark Coatings, Inc. listed as "a **North Carolina** corporation" (RSA: Ohio);
- Ridgewater Logistics, Inc. listed as "a **Delaware** corporation" (RSA: Georgia).
- **Impact:** Opinion coverage is jurisdiction-specific; misdescribed entities risk gaps in authority/enforceability coverage.

### 13. Backstop Commitment Agreement Date Inconsistency
- **New TL CA ("Backstop Parties"):** Backstop Commitment Agreement dated **May 12, 2025**.
- **SHA ("Backstop Parties"):** Backstop Commitment Agreement dated **May 30, 2025**.
- **Action Required:** Identify the operative agreement and conform references; the Backstop Commitment Agreement itself is not in the reviewed closing set — request a copy (it governs 55% of Pro Forma Equity).
