# Compliance Gap Report
## Ridgewater Holdings, Inc. — Restructuring Closing Document Review
### Prepared for the Ad Hoc Group of Term Loan Lenders (Stonebridge Capital Management; Clearfield Asset Partners, LP; Northvane Credit Opportunities Fund)
**Prepared by:** Ashworth, Beckett & Lowe LLP, Ad Hoc Group Counsel
**Date:** July 14, 2025 (Closing Date)
**Reference:** Restructuring Support Agreement dated April 28, 2025 (the "RSA") and Term Sheet (Exhibit A)

---

## I. Executive Summary

This report compares the executed closing deliverables against the RSA and Term Sheet from the perspective of the Ad Hoc Group of term loan lenders. The review identified **material discrepancies**, including two threshold closing-condition failures (minimum Exchange Offer participation; Environmental Solutions Sale net proceeds), pricing and maturity errors in the New TL Credit Agreement, an equity split/board composition in the Equity Shareholders Agreement that departs from the RSA, an MIP share reserve and vesting structure that violate the RSA, and multiple internal inconsistencies in the Officer's Certificate and Company Counsel Opinion.

**Critical items requiring immediate resolution or waiver by the Required Consenting Lenders:**
1. Minimum participation shortfall (94.34% vs. required 95%) — Section II.A
2. ES Sale Net Cash Proceeds likely below the $55,000,000 minimum — Section II.B
3. New TL pricing (SOFR + 500 bps vs. SOFR + 550 bps) — Section II.C
4. New 2L Notes maturity stated as April 28, 2030 (concurrent with the New TL) — Section II.C
5. Board composition contrary to RSA (2 Ad Hoc Group designees instead of 3) — Section II.E
6. MIP reserve of 9.5% and 60/40 allocation contrary to RSA's 8% cap and 50/50 allocation — Section II.F

---

## II. Detailed Findings

### A. Exchange Agreement — Minimum Participation Condition NOT Satisfied (CRITICAL)

- **RSA requirement:** "Minimum Participation Threshold" of **95%** of the outstanding Existing TLB (not less than $449,350,000) is a Condition Precedent (Section 7).
- **Exchange Agreement (Section 3.01):** Participating amount of **$446,200,000 = 94.34%**, leaving $26,800,000 (5.66%) held by Non-Exchanging Lenders. **The 95% threshold is not met.**
- **Officer's Certificate (Section 4(f)):** Certifies that holders of "not less than 95%" have tendered. **This certification is factually contradicted by the Exchange Agreement and appears to be inaccurate.** The Exchange Agent confirmation referenced in the Certificate must be produced and reconciled.
- **Consequence:** Absent a waiver by the Required Consenting Lenders, this is a failed Condition Precedent and a closing-condition default under the RSA.
- **Note:** All three Ad Hoc Group members tendered 100% ($340,500,000); the shortfall is attributable to non-group holders.

### B. Environmental Solutions Asset Purchase Agreement — Net Cash Proceeds (CRITICAL)

- **RSA requirement:** Environmental Solutions Sale Agreement must provide for **Net Cash Proceeds of not less than $55,000,000**. "Net Cash Proceeds" expressly deducts (i) transaction costs, (ii) taxes, and (iii) **escrow/holdback amounts until actually released**.
- **ES APA terms:** Purchase Price of $58,000,000, comprising a $53,800,000 Closing Cash Payment plus a **$4,200,000 escrow** (18-month Escrow Release Date), with Estimated Transaction Costs of **$1,100,000** plus taxes.
- **Computed Net Cash Proceeds at closing:** $58,000,000 − $4,200,000 (escrow) − $1,100,000 (costs) − taxes ≈ **$52.7 million or less — below the $55,000,000 minimum** even before tax deductions.
- **Officer's Certificate (Section 4(g)):** Certifies the $58,000,000 "purchase price" satisfies the $55,000,000 net-proceeds requirement. **This conflates gross purchase price with Net Cash Proceeds as defined in the RSA and appears incorrect.**
- **Additional issues:**
  - The ES APA Outside Date is **October 10, 2025** — the sale has not closed and may close well after the RSA Closing Date; the RSA-required binding agreement condition is met only if the proceeds test is satisfied.
  - The New TL Credit Agreement defines the "Environmental Solutions Asset Purchase Agreement" as **dated June 2, 2025**; the executed ES APA is **dated July 8, 2025**. Date discrepancy requiring correction.
  - The Company Counsel Opinion (Section 2, item 8) describes the ES APA as "dated as of the date hereof" (July 14, 2025) — inconsistent with both.
  - Assumed Liabilities include assumed trade payables up to $2,300,000 and certain environmental remediation obligations (Raleigh, NC and other locations) — environmental liability retention should be diligenced.

### C. New TL Credit Agreement — Pricing and Maturity Errors

- **Pricing:** Applicable Margin defined as **5.00% (500 bps)**. The RSA and Term Sheet require **SOFR + 550 bps**. The Exchange Agreement and Officer's Certificate both correctly recite 550 bps. **The New TL Credit Agreement must be corrected (a 50 bps economic deficiency on $280,000,000).**
- **New 2L Notes Maturity:** The New TL Credit Agreement defines the "New 2L Notes Maturity Date" as **April 28, 2030** — the same day as the New TL maturity. The RSA requires the New 2L Notes to mature **October 28, 2030** (six months after New TL maturity). This error, if left uncorrected, eliminates the intended 1L/2L maturity separation.
- **Consistent items:** $280,000,000 principal; April 28, 2030 New TL maturity; 66.67% Required Consenting Lenders definition; Subsidiary Guarantor list (7 domestic guarantors; Ridgewater Europe GmbH excluded) — all consistent with the RSA.
- **Intercompany claims:** Schedule 6.14 / $12,000,000 cap consistent with the RSA.

### D. New RCF Credit Agreement — Discrepancies

- **Closing Date draw:** The RSA specifies an initial Closing Date draw of **$40,000,000**; the New RCF requires a Closing Date Revolving Loan of **$47,000,000**. Departure from the Term Sheet (note the additional $7,000,000 is applied to refinance the $67,000,000 Existing RCF).
- **New 2L Notes principal:** The New RCF defines the New 2L Notes as **$210,000,000** aggregate principal; the RSA, Exchange Agreement, New TL Credit Agreement, and Officer's Certificate all state **$95,000,000**. Definition must be corrected.
- **Material Adverse Effect:** The New RCF contains its own broad MAE definition without the carve-outs in the New TL Credit Agreement. The RSA defines MAE by reference to the **New TL Credit Agreement**; the divergent RCF MAE may create inconsistencies in cross-defaults and closing conditions.
- **Commitment reduction:** Automatic 25% reduction of the Aggregate Revolving Commitment from ES Sale Net Cash Proceeds — not contemplated by the RSA/Term Sheet; effect on Minimum Liquidity testing should be assessed.
- **Consistent items:** $85,000,000 commitment; April 28, 2029 maturity; super-priority lien structure; Thorncastle as sole lender.

### E. Equity Shareholders Agreement — Governance and Equity Split

- **Board composition (Section 3.01(a)):** SHA provides **2 Ad Hoc Group designees, 3 Backstop Party designees**, 1 CEO Director, 1 Independent Director.
  - **RSA "New Board" requires 3 Ad Hoc Group designates (one each from Stonebridge, Clearfield, Northvane), 2 Backstop designees (only to the extent Backstop Parties include non-Ad Hoc Group members), 1 CEO, and 1 independent director mutually agreed by the Company and the Required Consenting Lenders.** The SHA reallocates a board seat from the Ad Hoc Group to the Backstop Parties — a material governance departure. This is of particular concern because the Backstop Parties appear to be the same institutions as the Ad Hoc Group; under the RSA, Backstop designees apply only to the extent the Backstop Parties include members **other than** the Ad Hoc Group.
  - Independent Director selection under the SHA requires Ad Hoc Group/Backstop agreement **plus CEO approval**; the RSA requires mutual agreement of the **Company and the Required Consenting Lenders**.
  - Designation thresholds (15%/25% on a Fully Diluted Basis) and seat-forfeiture mechanics are not contemplated by the RSA.
- **Equity split:** 55% Backstop / 30% Lender Shareholders / **15% Noteholder Shareholders plus warrants for an additional 5%** (strike at 1.5x plan equity value). The RSA contemplates cancellation of the Existing Notes; the Ad Hoc Group should confirm the 15% + 5% warrant package was in fact agreed, as it dilutes the lenders' 30% recovery. The warrants also interact with the SHA protective provisions and the MIP's Fully Diluted Basis calculation.
- **Backstop Commitment Agreement date:** The SHA references **May 30, 2025**; the New TL Credit Agreement references **May 12, 2025**. Discrepancy requiring reconciliation.

### F. Management Incentive Plan — Material Non-Compliance

- **Share reserve:** The MIP Executive Summary (Section 1) states 8%, but Sections 2.1 and 4.1 define the Aggregate Share Reserve as **9.5%** of Fully Diluted equity — **exceeding the RSA's hard cap of 8%** and internally inconsistent within the MIP itself.
- **Vesting allocation:** MIP allocates **60% time-based / 40% performance-based**; the RSA requires **50% / 50%**.
- **Vesting schedule:** Time-based awards vest over **3 years with a 6-month cliff**; the RSA requires vesting over **4 years with a 1-year cliff** (performance awards do use a 4-year/1-year structure).
- **Adoption/effectiveness:** The MIP Effective Date is a blank placeholder ("[●], 2025") — the plan does not appear to have been finally adopted as of the Closing Date, despite the Officer's Certificate (Section 4(j)) certifying due adoption.
- **Other:** Par value stated as $0.01 per share versus $0.001 in the Equity Shareholders Agreement; performance metrics are set by the Compensation Committee within 90 days post-closing subject only to RSA Section 7(j) form acceptance — confirm Required Consenting Lender approval of the plan as delivered (required given the deviations above).

### H. Company Counsel Legal Opinion — Inaccuracies

- **New 2L Notes description (Section 2, item 3):** Opinion describes the notes as "**11.50%/12.25%** Senior Secured Second Lien PIK Toggle Notes **due 2031**." All other documents (RSA, Exchange Agreement, New TL CA) state **10.00% cash / 12.00% PIK, due October 28, 2030**. The opinion describes instruments materially different from those contemplated by the RSA.
- **ES APA date:** Opinion references ES APA "dated as of the date hereof" (July 14, 2025); executed APA is dated July 8, 2025 (and the New TL CA says June 2, 2025). Three different dates across the closing set.
- **New TL principal:** Opinion correctly states $280,000,000, further confirming the Officer's Certificate's $285,000,000 is erroneous.
- **Scope limitations:** No opinion on German law (Ridgewater Europe GmbH) — consistent with the RSA's exclusion of Ridgewater Europe GmbH as a guarantor, but note no German-law coverage exists for this foreign subsidiary.

### I. Exchange Agreement — Subsidiary Guarantor Jurisdiction Errors

The Exchange Agreement's "Subsidiary Guarantors" definition misstates jurisdictions of incorporation:
- Ridgewater Industrial Services, Inc.: "a **Delaware** corporation" (RSA/other documents: **North Carolina**)
- Tidemark Coatings, Inc.: "a **North Carolina** corporation" (RSA/other documents: **Ohio**)
- Ridgewater Logistics, Inc.: "a **Delaware** corporation" (RSA/other documents: **Georgia**)

The New TL Credit Agreement, Equity Shareholders Agreement, Officer's Certificate, and Opinion all list the correct jurisdictions. The Exchange Agreement should be corrected to avoid perfection/enforceability ambiguity.

**Consistent items:** Exchange consideration ($280M 1L / $95M 2L / 30% equity; ~79.28% debt recovery plus equity); SOFR + 425 bps accrued interest cash payment; Section 3(a)(9) exemption; Outside Date July 15, 2025; milestone compliance (launch May 16 within May 19 deadline; expiration June 16 within June 18 deadline).

### J. Intercompany Claims

- Officer's Certificate (§4(n)): $11,800,000 subordinated — within the RSA's $12,000,000 cap, and subordination (rather than settlement) is a permitted RSA alternative. **Compliant**, subject to review of the subordination agreements' form and the Agent's satisfaction.

## IV. Recommended Actions

1. **Do not waive or treat as satisfied** the minimum participation condition (Item 1) without express written waiver by the Required Consenting Lenders and confirmation of the actual Exchange Agent report; $446,200,000 ≠ $449,350,000.
2. **Require correction or re-certificate** of the ES Sale proceeds analysis; if Net Cash Proceeds are below $55,000,000, either obtain a price increase, escrow reduction, or Required Consenting Lender waiver.
3. **Amend the New TL Credit Agreement** (or issue a correction letter) to fix the Applicable Margin (550 bps) and the New 2L Notes Maturity Date (October 28, 2030) prior to funding.
4. **Amend the New RCF** to correct the New 2L Notes principal ($95,000,000), harmonize the MAE definition with the New TL Credit Agreement, and conform the Closing Date draw or obtain lender consent to the $47,000,000 draw.
5. **Amend the Equity Shareholders Agreement** to restore the RSA board composition (3 Ad Hoc Group designees) and independent-director selection mechanics; reconcile the Backstop Commitment Agreement date.
6. **Amend the MIP** to an 8% reserve, 50/50 allocation, and 4-year/1-year-cliff vesting; complete execution with a definitive Effective Date.
7. **Obtain a corrected Officer's Certificate** (New TL $280M; New 2L $95M; corrected liquidity and participation computations) and a **corrected or supplemented legal opinion** accurately describing the New 2L Notes.
8. **Correct the Exchange Agreement's guarantor jurisdictions** via closing amendment or side letter.
