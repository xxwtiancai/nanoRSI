## IV. VOLUNTARY CONSENT

Pursuant to the Maryland Family Law Article, Title 5, Subtitle 3, I hereby **voluntarily and knowingly consent to the adoption** of my son, Elijah James Whitfield, by Marcus Holloway and Diana Holloway, and I consent to the entry of a decree of adoption by the Circuit Court for Baltimore City.

In giving this Consent, I affirm and acknowledge as follows:

1. **Voluntariness.** I execute this Consent freely and voluntarily, without coercion, duress, fraud, or undue influence of any kind. I have not received, and I was not promised, any compensation, payment, or thing of value in exchange for this Consent, other than permissible payments permitted by law.

2. **Independent Legal Advice.** I have been represented throughout by Danica Okafor, Esq., of my own choosing, and I have had the opportunity to consult with her concerning this Consent and its legal consequences before signing.

3. **Understanding of Consequences.** I understand that upon entry of a final decree of adoption, all parental rights I have with respect to Elijah James Whitfield will be terminated, including the right to custody, visitation, contact, and decision-making concerning the child; that I will have no legal relationship with the child; and that the child will thereafter be, for all legal purposes, the child of Marcus and Diana Holloway. I understand this Consent applies not only to the present adoption but to any future adoption of the child by the petitioners or their spouses.

4. **Understanding of Right to Rescind.** I have been advised by my counsel, and I understand, that under § 5-335 of the Maryland Family Law Article, a consent given before a parent is discharged from a facility or institution, or within thirty (30) days after execution, may be rescinded by written notice as provided by statute. **I have been fully informed of this statutory right of rescission notwithstanding my stated preference that the Consent be final and irrevocable immediately upon signing** (see accompanying drafting memorandum, Discrepancy No. 4). I further understand that this Consent becomes irrevocable, except for fraud, duress, or procedural defect, upon the earlier of (a) entry of an interlocutory decree of adoption, (b) the end of the applicable statutory rescission period, or (c) my affirmance in writing, in accordance with Md. Code Ann., Fam. Law § 5-335.

5. **Timing Relative to Treatment.** I affirm that I am not currently a patient or resident of any facility or institution as described in Md. Code Ann., Fam. Law § 5-336 (notice to facility where consenting parent is patient). I completed inpatient treatment at Harborview Recovery Center on January 15, 2024, and I currently attend weekly outpatient counseling. *(Attorney note: confirm § 5-336 notice analysis — see drafting memorandum, Open Item 3.)*

6. **Disclosure of Payments.** No person or agency has paid or promised to pay any amount to me, or on my behalf, for or in connection with the placement for adoption of the child, except as permitted by law, as more fully set forth in the accompanying Disclosure substantially in the form required by Md. Code Ann., Fam. Law § 5-338, executed simultaneously herewith.

7. **Notice and Opportunity.** I understand I may appear at the adoption hearing and that I may withdraw or rescind this Consent only in the manner and within the time permitted by law.

8. **Child Support and Assigned Rights.** I understand that I received Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF) benefits from the Maryland Department of Human Services from October 2021 through June 2023, and that my right to receive child support for the period covered by such assistance may have been assigned to the State of Maryland. I understand that this Consent does not itself discharge or satisfy any child support arrearages owed to the State or otherwise, and that any such arrearages must be addressed in the appropriate proceeding. *(See drafting memorandum, Discrepancy No. 5.)*

9. **Post-Adoption Contact.** I understand that Marcus and Diana Holloway and I have an informal understanding regarding future contact with Elijah, and that any post-adoption contact arrangement is not a condition of this Consent and is not enforceable as part of this Consent or the adoption decree unless separately formalized as provided by law. I acknowledge that I enter this Consent without regard to any such informal understanding. *(See drafting memorandum, Open Item 1.)*

## V. NOTICE REGARDING FINALITY

I acknowledge that the petitioners have requested that this Consent state that it is final and irrevocable immediately upon execution. I understand and have been advised by my counsel that **Maryland law does not permit the parties to waive or contract away the statutory right of rescission** under Md. Code Ann., Fam. Law § 5-335 by agreement; any provision purporting to do so would be unenforceable and could jeopardize the validity of the Consent.
