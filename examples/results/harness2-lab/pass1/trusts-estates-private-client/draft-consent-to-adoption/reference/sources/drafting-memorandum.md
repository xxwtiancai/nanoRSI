This memorandum accompanies the draft **Consent to Adoption by Biological Mother (Keisha R. Whitfield)** for your review and approval. The statutory framework is the Maryland Family Law Article, Title 5, Subtitle 3 (independent adoptions), principally §§ 5-335 (form and finality of consent; rescission), 5-336 (notice where parent is patient of facility), 5-337 (execution and acknowledgment of consent), and 5-338 (disclosure of payments).

## II. Key Drafting Decisions

1. **Consent drafted to conform to § 5-335 requirements**, including express advisement of the statutory rescission right. Keisha's stated wish (per intake memo) that the consent be "final and irrevocable immediately" **cannot be honored as drafted**, because Maryland law does not permit waiver of the § 5-335 rescission right by private agreement. The draft instead includes an explicit advisement (¶¶ IV.4, V) documenting that she was informed of the right notwithstanding her preference. This protects the consent's validity.

2. **Execution venue.** Per § 5-337, consent must be executed in the presence of a notary or acknowledged before the court. The draft provides for notarized execution at our office, with a note to consider execution before the Judge/Master. Recommend confirming whether Danica Okafor will attend (open client question).

3. **Payments disclosure (§ 5-338).** Intake states no compensation or inducement was provided; the draft includes a disclosure section to be completed and signed at execution.

4. **Post-adoption contact.** The informal visitation understanding ("a few times a year," holidays/birthdays) is expressly recited as *not* a condition of the consent and unenforceable unless separately formalized. Recommendation: keep any post-adoption contact arrangement out of the consent; if the parties want enforceability, a separate post-adoption contact agreement should be prepared (see Open Item 1).

5. **Father's consent.** Darnell Tyrone Whitfield executed a Consent and Relinquishment of Parental Rights on March 1, 2024, witnessed and notarized at Jessup Correctional Institution, per intake and home study. A copy must be obtained from Gerald Tate, Esq., and independently verified before relying on it (Open Item 2).
