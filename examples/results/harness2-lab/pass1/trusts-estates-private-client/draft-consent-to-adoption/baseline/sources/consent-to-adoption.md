## CONSENT TO ADOPTION AND WAIVER OF NOTICE

I, **Keisha R. Whitfield**, of full age, being first duly sworn upon my oath, depose and state as follows:

### 1. Identity of Consenting Parent

1.1 I am the biological mother of **Elijah James Whitfield**, a minor child, born **March 17, 2017** *(see Section 10 regarding a date-of-birth discrepancy in the file)*, at Lakeview Regional Medical Center, Baltimore, Maryland, Birth Certificate No. 2017-03-127845, issued by the Maryland Division of Vital Records.

1.2 My date of birth is September 2, 1993. My address is 1410 East Preston Street, Apt. 3B, Baltimore, Maryland 21213.

1.3 I am not now married, and I have never been married, to the child's biological father, Darnell Tyrone Whitfield, who is named as the father on the child's birth certificate.

### 2. Identity of Prospective Adoptive Parents

2.1 I hereby freely and voluntarily give my consent to the adoption of my son, Elijah James Whitfield, by **Marcus Holloway** and **Diana Holloway (née Whitfield)**, husband and wife, residing at 2847 Oriole Nest Lane, Towson, Maryland 21204. Diana Holloway is the child's biological paternal aunt.

2.2 A Petition for Adoption was filed by the prospective adoptive parents on March 20, 2024, in the Circuit Court for Baltimore City (Case No. 24-A-0001537), presently assigned to the Honorable Patricia Langford.

### 3. Voluntariness of Consent

3.1 I execute this consent of my own free will and accord, without coercion, duress, fraud, misrepresentation, or undue influence of any kind.

3.2 No payment, compensation, financial inducement, or thing of value has been offered or given to me, or to anyone on my behalf, in exchange for this consent.

3.3 I am represented by independent counsel of my own choosing, Danica Okafor, Esq., of Okafor Legal Services LLC, 305 East Fayette Street, Suite 200, Baltimore, Maryland 21202, who has advised me of my legal rights and of the consequences of signing this consent. *(Signature block below provides for certification that my attorney was present, per Md. Code, Fam. Law § 5-335 et seq.; presence to be confirmed.)*

3.4 I understand that I have the right to counsel and, if eligible, to court-appointed counsel, and that I may revoke this consent within the period permitted by Maryland law.

### 4. Understanding of Legal Effect

4.1 **I understand that by signing this consent, I permanently and irrevocably relinquish all parental rights to Elijah James Whitfield**, including the rights to custody, visitation, communication, and decision-making concerning the child, upon entry of a decree of adoption.

4.2 I understand that upon the entry of the final decree of adoption, I will have no legal relationship with, and no legal rights or obligations concerning, the child, except as may be provided by law regarding inheritance.

4.3 I understand that the child has been residing with the prospective adoptive parents since August 3, 2023, pursuant to a Temporary Guardianship Order entered in Case No. 23-G-0004218, Circuit Court for Baltimore City, and that a favorable home study was completed April 12, 2024, by Carolyn Voss, LCSW, of Brightpath Family Services LLC.

4.4 I understand that the biological father, Darnell Tyrone Whitfield, executed a Consent and Relinquishment of Parental Rights on March 1, 2024, while incarcerated at Jessup Correctional Institution, and that his counsel is Gerald Tate, Esq. **This consent does not resolve, release, or discharge any child support obligation or arrearages owed by either biological parent**, which remain subject to the existing support order in Case No. 19-FS-0008714 and applicable law, including any assignment of rights to the State of Maryland arising from receipt of TANF benefits.

### 5. Statement Concerning Timing and Revocation

5.1 I have expressed a personal wish that this consent be final and irrevocable immediately upon signing, without any waiting or revocation period. I understand that **Maryland law governs the timing and revocability of this consent and that a statutory revocation window may apply notwithstanding my wishes**. I acknowledge that my counsel has explained to me the exact revocation period applicable under Maryland law and that I may not waive statutory rights that are non-waivable.

### 6. Post-Adoption Contact

6.1 I understand that the prospective adoptive parents and I have discussed an informal understanding that I may have occasional contact with the child, such as visits around holidays and birthdays. **I understand and acknowledge that any such contact arrangement is not a condition of this consent, is not enforceable as part of the adoption proceeding, and does not affect the validity or finality of this consent.** Any enforceable post-adoption contact agreement, if desired, must be entered into separately as permitted by Maryland law.

### 7. Waiver of Notice

7.1 To the extent permitted by Maryland law, I waive notice of all further proceedings in this adoption matter, including any hearing on the Petition for Adoption, and I do not intend to appear or object.

### 8. Certification of Acknowledgment

8.1 I acknowledge that I execute this consent before a notary public or other officer authorized to administer oaths, and that my signature below is given freely and voluntarily.

### 9. No Prior Proceedings

9.1 I acknowledge that no prior adoption proceeding has been filed with respect to the child, other than the pending Petition for Adoption in Case No. 24-A-0001537.
