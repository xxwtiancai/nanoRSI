## I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The draft 8-K requires **substantial revision before filing**. The most significant deficiencies are:

1. **Missing Item 2.03** (direct financial obligation — $15,000,000 draw and $20,000,000 commitment increase). *(ACTION REQUIRED per checklist.)*
2. **Missing Item 3.02** (unregistered sales of equity securities — three separate issuances: PIPE shares, Ridgeline stock consideration, and placement agent warrants). *(ACTION REQUIRED per checklist.)*
3. **Wrong Item number for officer changes** — the draft uses Item 5.01 (Changes in Control); the CFO departure/appointment belongs under **Item 5.02**. No change of control occurred.
4. **No standalone Item 2.01** caption for the completed acquisition.
5. **Exhibit index errors** — Brennan Separation Agreement and Placement Agent Warrant are missing; the Lindqvist agreement is mislabeled and mis-numbered (10.3 vs. 10.6).
6. **Item 9.01(a)/(b)** — no treatment of Rule 3-05 target financial statements or Article 11 pro forma information.
7. **Multiple factual discrepancies** against the deal documents (share count, credit agreement terms, Trevino reporting line, equipment location, covenant history), detailed below.
