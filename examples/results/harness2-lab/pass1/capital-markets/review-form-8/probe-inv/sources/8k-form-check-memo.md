## I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The draft Form 8-K covers the APA acquisition (Item 1.01), CFO transition, and the press release (Item 7.01), and includes an exhibit index. However, the draft has **material deficiencies that must be corrected before filing**:

1. **Missing Item 2.03** — no standalone disclosure of the $15,000,000 revolver draw / $50,000,000 commitment increase (required).
2. **Missing Item 3.02** — no disclosure of the three unregistered issuances (PIPE shares, Ridgeline stock consideration, Placement Agent Warrants) (required).
3. **Wrong item number for CFO transition** — draft uses Item 5.01 (Changes in Control of Registrant); it must be **Item 5.02**. No change of control occurred, and the checklist confirms Item 5.01 is not triggered. The error also propagates to the cover-page item list.
4. **Item 2.01** — checklist treats the closing of the acquisition as triggering Item 2.01 (combined with Item 1.01); the draft does not caption an Item 2.01 heading. Add or confirm combined-disclosure treatment.
5. **Exhibit index errors** — Exhibit 10.3 is mislabeled (Lindqvist "Employment Agreement"); the Brennan Separation Agreement (10.3), Form of Registration Rights Agreement (10.4), and Form of Placement Agent Warrant (10.5) are missing or misnumbered.
6. **Item 9.01(a)/(b)** — draft states "Not applicable" without addressing Rule 3-05 significance testing or a commitment to file Ridgeline financial statements and Article 11 pro forma information by amendment (71-day deadline, ~September 16, 2025).
7. **Factual discrepancies vs. source documents** — stock consideration share count (draft: 1,500,000; deal documents: 1,562,500); Trevino reporting line (draft: CEO; APA summary: CTO); RSU plan year (draft: 2021 Plan; resolutions: 2022 Plan); Amendment No. 1 date (draft: June 30, 2024; resolutions/term sheet: March 3, 2024); leverage covenant description (draft says Amendment No. 2 increased it from 3.00x to 3.50x; term sheet and resolutions say 3.50x unchanged, with a new $10M minimum liquidity covenant); cover-page share count should be updated to post-transaction 44,625,000.

## II. ITEM-BY-ITEM ANALYSIS

### Item 1.01 — Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement

**Triggered: Yes. Addressed: Yes, but with errors and gaps.**

**What the draft does correctly:**
- Describes the APA dated July 7, 2025 with Ridgeline Data Systems, LLC, simultaneous signing and closing, the OrchestraAI platform, IP, customer contracts, and equipment.
- Describes the Purchase Price components ($28,000,000 cash; $12,500,000 stock; up to $7,000,000 earnout), working capital adjustment mechanics, assumed liabilities ($2,300,000 trade payables + $1,100,000 deferred revenue = $3,400,000), representations/warranties and survival periods, indemnification basket ($475,000) and cap ($14,250,000 / 30%), 12-month lock-up, Trevino non-compete (3 years, US/Canada), and Exhibit 2.1 incorporation-by-reference language with the Item 601(b)(2) omitting-schedules statement.

**Issues identified:**

1. **Stock Consideration share count error.** Draft states 1,500,000 shares at $8.00 (≈$12,500,000). The APA summary, SPA summary, board resolutions, and press release all state **1,562,500 shares** ($12,500,000 ÷ $8.00). The draft figure is also internally inconsistent (1,500,000 × $8.00 = $12,000,000, not $12,500,000). Correct throughout.
2. **Earnout payment mechanics.** Draft says payment is due within 30 business days of milestone achievement, measured "at any time during" the 12-/24-month period. The APA summary provides for certification within 30 days and payment within 30 days after certification, with milestones independently measured (Milestone 2 achievable without Milestone 1). The draft's statement that a missed milestone is extinguished, and its stand-alone ARR carve-out, are consistent with the APA summary, but verify the "30 business days" timing against the executed APA and correct if needed.
3. **Trevino reporting line.** Draft says Trevino reports to the CEO; the APA summary states he reports to the **Chief Technology Officer**. Correct (verify against executed APA).
4. **Survival periods.** Draft states 18 months general, 36 months fundamental, and **24 months for IP representations**. The APA summary states only 18 months general and 36 months fundamental, with no separate 24-month IP tier. Verify against the executed APA; if the 24-month IP tier is not in the APA, delete it.
5. **Indemnification reciprocity.** Draft says Company obligations are subject to "comparable limitations" — verify against the executed APA.
6. **Speculative/forward-looking content.** The paragraph on "revenue synergies," ARR trajectory, and 350+ enterprise customers is projection-type disclosure more suited to the furnished press release than filed Item 1.01 text. Recommend deleting or moving to Item 7.01 to avoid filed forward-looking statements without safe-harbor protection.
7. **Credit facility (Amendment No. 2) description.** Errors within the "Funding of the Acquisition" section:
   - **Amendment No. 1 date:** draft says June 30, 2024; the board resolutions and credit amendment term sheet say **March 3, 2024**. Correct.
   - **Leverage covenant:** draft states Amendment No. 2 increased the maximum leverage ratio from 3.00:1.00 to 3.50:1.00 with a step-down. The term sheet and board resolutions state the **3.50:1.00 maximum net leverage ratio is unchanged**; the covenant Amendment No. 2 actually added is the **$10,000,000 minimum liquidity covenant**. Correct this description.
   - **Interest rate:** draft says "SOFR plus 2.75%" — the term sheet specifies **Term SOFR + 2.75%** (increased from +2.25%), with a 0.10% credit spread adjustment. Refine.
   - A fuller treatment is required under Item 2.03 (see below); the Item 1.01 description should cross-reference rather than duplicate.
8. **SPA treatment.** The SPA (562,500 shares at $16.00; $9,000,000 gross; three accredited investors; executed July 3, 2025; closed July 7, 2025) is mentioned only as a funding source and listed as Exhibit 10.2. It is a material definitive agreement and should be described in Item 1.01 (parties, shares, price, exemption, lock-up, registration rights, placement agent compensation), cross-referenced to the new Item 3.02 section. Note: the SPA summary states a Form D must be filed by July 22, 2025 — a related but separate obligation outside the 8-K.

**Funding math note:** the draft describes sources as $15,000,000 credit draw + ~$4,000,000 cash + $9,000,000 private placement. Gross proceeds were $9,000,000, but net proceeds were approximately $8,475,000 after the $450,000 placement fee and ~$75,000 of expenses (SPA summary; the APA summary's $8,550,000 figure nets only the fee). State gross vs. net precisely and confirm against the funds-flow.

### Item 2.03 — Creation of a Direct Financial Obligation

**Triggered: Yes. Addressed: NO — MISSING (ACTION REQUIRED).**

The $15,000,000 draw on July 7, 2025 and the $20,000,000 commitment increase clearly trigger Item 2.03. Per the checklist, a standalone Item 2.03 section must disclose:
- Amount of the obligation ($15,000,000 drawn; $50,000,000 total commitment after the increase from $30,000,000);
- Material terms: Term SOFR + 2.75% (plus 0.10% credit spread adjustment), interest quarterly in arrears, maturity September 15, 2027, unused line fee 0.25%, amendment fee $100,000 and upfront fee $100,000;
- Financial covenants: minimum liquidity of $10,000,000 (new), maximum net leverage ratio 3.50:1.00 (unchanged), minimum Consolidated EBITDA $8,000,000;
- Events of default (financial covenant breach with no cure period; cross-default to indebtedness over $2,000,000; change of control at 35% beneficial ownership; judgments over $3,000,000; etc.);
- The original Credit Agreement dated September 15, 2023, as amended by Amendment No. 1 dated March 3, 2024;
- First-priority security interest in substantially all assets and the 30-day post-closing supplemental security agreement covering the acquired OrchestraAI IP.

Exhibit 10.1 is already listed and ties to this item; the disclosure section itself must be added.

### Item 3.02 — Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities

**Triggered: Yes. Addressed: NO — MISSING (ACTION REQUIRED).**

Three unregistered issuances must be disclosed, none of which currently appears under Item 3.02:

1. **PIPE:** 562,500 shares at $16.00/share; $9,000,000 gross proceeds; purchasers Clearwater Growth Fund IV, LP (250,000 shares / $4,000,000), Belmont Ventures Partners, LLC (187,500 / $3,000,000), Tidewater Capital Advisors, LP (125,000 / $2,000,000); SPA dated July 3, 2025; closing July 7, 2025; Section 4(a)(2) / Rule 506(b); accredited investors; no general solicitation; 6-month lock-up (expires January 7, 2026); registration rights (demand after 6 months; piggyback).
2. **Ridgeline stock consideration:** 1,562,500 shares at a deemed $8.00/share ($12,500,000); Section 4(a)(2) / Rule 506(b); Ridgeline (accredited through sole member Marcus Trevino); restricted securities with legends; 12-month lock-up (expires July 7, 2026).
3. **Placement Agent Warrants:** Calloway Marsh Capital LLC; warrants for 28,125 shares at a $16.00 exercise price, 5-year term (through July 7, 2030), cashless exercise; issued under Section 4(a)(2); **not disclosed anywhere in the draft**. Also disclose the $450,000 cash placement fee (5.0% of gross proceeds).

For each issuance, Item 3.02 requires the date of sale, title and amount of securities, identity of purchasers, aggregate offering price, and the exemption relied upon. Additional flags: the PIPE price ($16.00) and the deemed Ridgeline price ($8.00, described in the APA summary as a negotiated discount to market) raise a Nasdaq Listing Rule 5635 shareholder-approval / pricing question the source documents do not address — flag for outside counsel; also confirm any required Nasdaq Listing of Additional Shares notification. Aggregate new issuances (562,500 PIPE + 28,125 warrant shares ≈ 1.4% of 42,500,000 pre-transaction shares) are small relative to outstanding shares.

### Item 9.01 — Financial Statements and Exhibits

**Triggered: Yes. Addressed: Partially (ACTION REQUIRED on (a) and (b); exhibit index errors).**

**(a) Financial Statements of Businesses Acquired.** Draft says "Not applicable." This is premature. Rule 3-05 of Regulation S-X requires audited Ridgeline financial statements if any significance test (investment, asset, or income) exceeds 20%. The checklist notes the investment test appears well below 20% (~7% vs. ~$680M market cap), but the **asset and income tests have not been completed**. Options: (1) include financial statements now; (2) state they will be filed by amendment no later than 71 calendar days after the initial 8-K is due (~September 16, 2025); or (3) if all tests are below 20%, document the significance analysis and retain "Not applicable." Obtain significance data and outside counsel sign-off (Whitfield & Crane LLP — Diana Wexford) before leaving (a) blank.

**(b) Pro Forma Financial Information.** Same analysis — Article 11 pro forma information is required if (a) is required, with the same 71-day amendment window. The draft's "Not applicable" must be supported.

**(c) Shell Company Transactions.** "Not applicable" — correct; NovaPulse is not a shell company.

**(d) Exhibits.** See Part III.
