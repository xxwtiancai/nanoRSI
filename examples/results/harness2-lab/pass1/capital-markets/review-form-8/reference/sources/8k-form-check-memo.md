## I. Scope of Review

This memorandum reviews the draft Form 8-K dated July 8, 2025 against: (1) the Asset Purchase Agreement summary (apa-summary.docx); (2) the Securities Purchase Agreement summary (spa-summary.docx); (3) the Amendment No. 2 credit facility term sheet (credit-amendment-termsheet.docx); (4) the Board unanimous written consent dated July 6, 2025 (board-resolutions.docx); (5) the press release dated July 7, 2025 (press-release.docx); and (6) the internal 8-K items and exhibit checklists (filing-checklist.xlsx).

**Overall conclusion:** The draft is **not ready to file**. It contains three missing required Items (2.01 as a separately identified item, 2.03, and 3.02), a mislabeled Item (5.01 should be 5.02), factual discrepancies against the deal documents (stock consideration share count, Amendment No. 1 date, interest rate history, leverage covenant treatment), incomplete Item 9.01 financial statement treatment, exhibit index errors, and cover page issues. Required corrections are summarized in Section X.

## II. Item-by-Item Review

### Item 1.01 — Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement — **PARTIAL; REVISE**

The APA disclosure is generally thorough, but the following discrepancies and gaps must be corrected:

1. **Stock consideration share count is WRONG.** The draft states 1,500,000 shares at $8.00/share. The APA summary, Board resolutions, and press release all confirm **1,562,500 shares** ($12,500,000 ÷ $8.00 = 1,562,500). This error appears in the Purchase Price section and propagates to the lock-up description and share-count math. **Correct throughout.**
2. **Equipment location is WRONG.** The draft states hardware is located at Ridgeline's data center in **Raleigh, North Carolina**. The APA summary places Ridgeline's principal offices and the tangible assets at **Boulder, Colorado** (Schedule 2.1 assets located at the Boulder, CO facility). Correct.
3. **Patent/IP detail unverified.** The draft recites "three issued United States patents and two pending patent applications" and "approximately 78 active contracts." These figures do not appear in the APA summary or Board resolutions (which reference only Schedules 5.1(d) and (e)). Verify against the executed APA schedules or delete.
4. **Earnout mechanics imprecise.** The draft describes milestones measured "at any time during" the 12-/24-month periods and characterizes Milestone 2 as an "additional" installment. Per the APA summary, each milestone is measured independently (Milestone 2 does not require prior achievement of Milestone 1), and payments are due within 30 days of delivery of a milestone certification. The draft's statement that Milestone 2 is an "installment" following Milestone 1 is inconsistent; revise to reflect independent measurement. Also add that ARR methodology is defined in Schedule 3.3 and that Graystone & Whitaker, P.C. may not serve as dispute-resolution firm.
5. **Survival periods partially inconsistent.** The draft adds IP representations surviving 24 months; the APA summary specifies only 18 months (general) and 36 months (fundamental). Confirm against the executed APA or conform to the summary.
6. **Indemnification figures unverified.** The $475,000 basket and $14,250,000 cap (30%) do not appear in the reviewed documents. Verify against the executed APA.
7. **Trevino reporting line discrepancy.** The draft states Mr. Trevino will report to the **CEO**; the APA summary states he will report to the **Chief Technology Officer**. Confirm and conform.
8. **Working capital adjustment reference.** The draft cites "Section 2.06 of the APA" and a 90-day final determination; the APA summary does not corroborate this section reference. Verify.
9. **Forward-looking synergy paragraph.** The paragraph in Item 1.01 regarding "significant revenue synergies" and ARR growth is promotional forward-looking language. It appears in the furnished press release (Exhibit 99.1) but should not appear in a *filed* Item without safe-harbor/forward-looking statement cautionary language. Recommend deletion from the filed disclosure (it is adequately covered in Item 7.01/Exhibit 99.1) or addition of appropriate cautionary language.
10. **SPA treatment inadequate.** The Securities Purchase Agreement (executed July 3, 2025; closed July 7, 2025; 562,500 shares at $16.00/share; $9,000,000 gross proceeds) is itself a material definitive agreement. It is mentioned only in passing in the funding paragraph. Either describe it under Item 1.01 with cross-reference to the new Item 3.02 disclosure, or reference Item 3.02. The Registration Rights Agreement (demand rights after 6 months; piggyback rights) should also be referenced.
11. **APA boilerplate.** The "no longer operative" termination sentence and the standard non-reliance/limited-purpose language are acceptable; retained.

### Missing Item 2.03 — Creation of a Direct Financial Obligation — **ADD (ACTION REQUIRED)**

The $15,000,000 draw on July 7, 2025 and the $20,000,000 commitment increase clearly trigger Item 2.03. The draft relegates the credit facility to the Item 1.01 funding paragraph. Add a standalone Item 2.03 section disclosing:

- **Obligation:** $15,000,000 drawn under the amended revolving facility on July 7, 2025; revolving commitment increased from $30,000,000 to $50,000,000.
- **Rate:** Term SOFR + 2.75% per annum (increased from SOFR + 2.25% by Amendment No. 2), plus 0.10% credit spread adjustment; default rate +2.00%; interest payable quarterly in arrears.
- **Maturity:** September 15, 2027 (unchanged).
- **Fees:** 0.25% unused line fee; $100,000 amendment fee and $100,000 upfront fee on the incremental commitment paid at effectiveness.
- **Financial covenants:** NEW minimum liquidity covenant of $10,000,000 (added by Amendment No. 2); maximum net leverage ratio 3.50:1.00; minimum consolidated EBITDA $8,000,000 (trailing four quarters), each tested quarterly.
- **Security:** first-priority security interest in substantially all assets; supplemental security agreement covering Ridgeline-acquired assets (including OrchestraAI IP) due within 30 days of the Amendment Effective Date.
- **Events of default (material ones):** no cure period for financial covenant breach; cross-default to other indebtedness exceeding $2,000,000; change of control (35% beneficial ownership / delisting); judgments over $3,000,000.

**Factual corrections to the draft's credit facility description:**
- The draft states Amendment No. 1 was dated **June 30, 2024**; the term sheet and Board resolutions state **March 3, 2024**. Correct.
- The draft states the leverage covenant was increased from 3.00:1.00 to 3.50:1.00 by Amendment No. 2 with a step-down; the term sheet and Board resolutions state the 3.50:1.00 maximum net leverage ratio was **already in effect and unchanged** by Amendment No. 2. Correct.
- The draft describes the rate as simply "SOFR plus 2.75%" without noting it was increased from SOFR + 2.25% or the 0.10% spread adjustment; conform.
- The draft omits the new minimum liquidity covenant — a key term of Amendment No. 2 — and the minimum EBITDA covenant. Add.
- The draft's characterization of the general covenants ("minimum liquidity requirements, restrictions on additional indebtedness...") is too generic; replace with the Item 2.03 disclosure above.

### Item 9.01 — Financial Statements and Exhibits — **REVISE (ACTION REQUIRED)**

If any test is met, the draft must either include the audited Ridgeline financial statements or state that they **will be filed by amendment not later than 71 calendar days after the date the initial 8-K must be filed** (approximately September 16, 2025). The blanket "Not applicable" is not supportable without documented significance testing.
2. **Subsection (b):** Same analysis for Article 11 pro forma financial information; include the 71-day undertaking if required.
3. **Subsection (c):** "Not applicable" (shell company) is correct.
